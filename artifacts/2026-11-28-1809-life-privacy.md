# Life privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026. Life is an open-source app: [github.com/eric-edouard/life](https://github.com/eric-edouard/life).

## Where your data lives

Life runs on your own computer. Everything it reads and everything it builds from it is stored there. The developer runs no server that receives your data.

## Google data

With your consent, Life reads Gmail (read-only), Google Calendar, Google Contacts and Google Tasks through Life's own Google OAuth client. The sign-in tokens are stored on your computer only. Google data is never sent to the developer and never sold.

Life's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Life uses Google data only to provide its memory features to you, and humans do not read it. The one place it can reach a third party is the AI provider you choose, below.

## Third parties and what they receive

Nothing else leaves your computer. Each item can be turned off.

- **AI model provider you choose.** The text Life reads (including text from the sources you turned on, which may include Google data) goes to the provider you pick: Claude or Codex through your own subscription, or OpenRouter or allmodels.io with your key. The default route for new users is OpenRouter with zero data retention, so only providers that keep nothing serve the request. Images in your messages are sent once, for description.
- **Speech to text.** Voice notes are transcribed on your computer. Some speech (for example Thai) is sent to ElevenLabs for transcription.
- **Web search and Apify.** If you give your agents a search key, their search queries go to Tavily, Serper, Brave Search or Perplexity. If you give Life an Apify token, store searches go to Apify, and a run only on your approval.
- **Messages you approve.** A message is sent only as the exact draft you approve, through WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, LinkedIn, Messages, Gmail or GitHub.
- **Phone link.** If you pair a phone, connection metadata (IP addresses, device keys, timing) passes through a public relay run by n0 (iroh). Content is encrypted end to end. Phone notifications go through Apple's push service and carry opaque ids only.
- **Email to you.** Reports and alerts you turn on are sent through Resend.
- **Fonts and downloads.** The sign-in page loads a font from Google Fonts. Model files, tools and updates download from Hugging Face and GitHub. These see your IP address and nothing else.

The full list is in [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/eric-edouard/life/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Your control

You choose the sources, the provider and the integrations. You can delete anything from Life's memory, and removing Life from your computer removes its data. You can revoke Life's access at any time in your Google Account permissions.
