# Life

Life is a self-running memory of your own life. It reads your messages, mail, notes, calendar, voice recordings and more, and turns them into a searchable record of your days, people and projects.

It runs on your own computer. Your data stays there, and you decide which sources it reads and which AI provider it may use.

Life is open source: [github.com/eric-edouard/life](https://github.com/eric-edouard/life)
