hostedbyme.app

Each address under this domain, like k7f3q2.hostedbyme.app, is one person's own server, usually the Life app running on their Mac. This domain only relays the encrypted connection to it: the relay holds no certificate for those addresses and cannot read what passes through.

Report abuse (phishing, malware, spam) at an address under this domain: abuse@hostedbyme.app. Include the full address; reports are answered within a few days.

Source: github.com/eric-edouard/life.
