# apps/ios

`Lifelog/` is the SwiftUI app: one `@Observable` store per feature, the long-lived ones built at launch. `Kit/` is the
Swift package with everything that doesn't need UIKit, so it builds and tests on the Mac in seconds. `Watch/` and
`Widgets/` are the glances; `Shared/` holds code and copy that the app and its extensions share. `project.yml`
generates the gitignored `.xcodeproj`.
The app is Life's three tabs, Inbox (`Inbox/`: the `CheckCard`s, paged by swipe, and its `AnswerResultView`; Life's own asks (`misread` set) end with a compact "Wrong…" at the bottom right, which opens Ask Life on that card (`AppRouter.askLife(about:)`, a context chip: his complaint goes through Ask Life alone); a card the caretaker is fixing (`beingFixed`) says so under its tile and in its list row; every ask is one `CheckCard` (decision 98): its matter chip above the question, the thing at full length, its "Why …" link under it, and its answers in one zone, the first filled (a plan's `start`, `Check.optionsInOrder`) then "Tell the agent…" and the quiet ones; a plan card's steps (`PlanBlock`, from `Check.planSteps`, whatever `isPlan` says) show his own with the white dot in bold and the agent's with its program mark, dimmer, each ending with where it happens, then the source chip (`gathered`) that opens the context in place of the "Why" link; one ask on show is `CardView`, hosted by the Inbox and by a matter's stack (`AppRouter.matterPath`); the Inbox is a `NavigationSplitView`: the list of asks (`CheckList`: what blocks first, then "The rest can wait" over what only improves; answers in flight, or confirmed after the read shown was asked are left out (`LifeStore.answersNewer`, `AnsweredCards`): a read asked later says what is open; its Filter menu (`FilterMenu`, via `filterBar`: a top-leading toolbar item, or a round glass button floating at the list's bottom left in the closed Duo's side column, so the top group stays the Pendant and Settings) keeps one kind of ask (`CheckGroup`, per screen state, never stored) in the list and the pager alike: `InboxOrder.only`) beside the one ask on show, whose swipe is a paged `LazyHStack`; every answer on its own card that is not a send or a goal edit (a start included: "Starting…", then "Started") shows on the button he pressed (working, then Life's green word for 0.6 s, then the next ask; not done, the ask open again with Life's reason above its answers: `CheckActions.pressed`), and a bulk card's or the moves list's answers are sent at once, their rows working until Life answers (`LifeStore.answerRows`; its word is said in the toast, an overlay at the top of the ask, or the closed Duo's side-column capsule `ColumnToast` for what has an Undo, by `reportsSideColumn`)) and Projects (`Matters/`, decision 95: `ProjectsScreen`, every running matter ranked under a row of chips, All then `read_projects`' chips in Life's order, each its icon, name, flag dot and spinner, the parked ones dimmed; a chip filters in place by Life's own read of its list (`read_matters` of the project, or of the area with `noProject`; the phone sorts and filters nothing), the chosen chip in `AppRouter.chip`, reset to All by tapping the tab again; a project's chip shows its header (`ProjectHead`, `read_project`: where it stands, status and goal with Details behind the info button, its question as the one coral card that opens the card in a sheet once its matter's read holds it (`ProjectAsk`), its agents); a long press on a project's chip offers Park, Mark done and Details; the agents (`AgentsScreen`, from Settings' "Your agents") and an agent's page (`AgentPage`: its matters by Needs you, Waiting on someone and In work, from Kit's `HeldMatters`; Runs on; What wakes it, the agent's sheet) show in the detail column too (`AppRouter.Detail.agents`), an agent's page pushed on whatever page is on show (`AppRouter.open(agent:)`, any agent's name or row), a matter from it pushed on it;  Details (`ProjectDetails`) shows in the detail column, the one destination it shares with a matter (`AppRouter.Detail`): status as one control, the goal it serves, people, chats and repos, the last two shown, never opened, until Life's read gives a link (life#928); on All, his marked matters first and a parked project's last under "Parked", a swipe to the right marks one; a matter's page, its mark in the bar and its project a link to its chip; a matter's icon down one chain, his pick, Life's, the first area's, the folder (`MatterRow.symbol`, Kit: Life sends the first two), its grid opened from the page's meta line (`MatterIconSheet`); his mark and a project's status are sent at once; they and a matter's icon are said back with Undo, the opposite change: `LifeStore.saidBack`) and People (`People/`: everyone Life knows, closest first or A to Z, all, people or organizations, `PeopleList.groups` in Kit; a person's or organization's page from their `now`, which every party Life names opens (`AppRouter.open(party:)`): who, reach (a tile opens the one chat or call, or the system menu of their accounts), what is open with them, lately, details, what Life knows and their latest exchanges, each part a page pushed on them (`AppRouter.personPath`; prose parts parsed by Kit's `Prose`); the head picture opens the picture sheet when he can pick one (`PicturesSheet`: Life's kept pictures, one per source, and none; a tap works until Life took it and the read shows it, then "Picture changed" with Undo, `LifeStore.setPicture`, his directory's `pictureFrom` through `change_directory_entry`); an organization's page, `OrgPage`, from the same read: the people he knows there, now then Before, each opening their page, its matters, its last exchange and its domains).
The bars are two groups of system pieces, declared and never drawn
(`Shell/TopBar.swift`): on top the Pendant's icon and the Settings gear (Settings only from Inbox and Projects; a pushed
page has Back and the Pendant; a sheet only its Close: one head row in the content, its title left and Close right (`SheetHeader`, `CloseButton`), the
bar of its `sheetStack()` hidden, and `CloseItem` in the bar only for the Pendant sheet),
at the bottom the tabs. A screen is one full-height scroll with nothing scrolling inside it, and its answers float over it in the bottom `safeAreaBar` (decision 97; an ask's answers that can grow tall, people to pick or an accessibility text size, end its scroll instead). A sheet does the same through `sheetScroll`: its head in the top bar, its buttons in the bottom bar. A list screen's name is its `screenTitle` (`DesignSystem/ScreenTitle.swift`, decision 84): the name and one muted state word ("Projects 3 underway"), never a sentence, on the system's `safeAreaBar` with its soft scroll edge, never a navigation title, except on the open Duo (a regular window), where it is iOS's inline title centred in the top bar (his ask of 22 Nov); every scroll in a tab runs edge to edge under the floating bars, the soft edge set once on `RootView` (a sheet sets its own); on Inbox, Projects, People, Live logs and Spending, and on Agents, Notifications and Last night when they are built. A page whose first line is its name (a matter, a person, an agent) and a sheet have none. Sheets are centred, the system's
default: no `presentationPlacement` (`.leading` is missing on iOS 27.0 and crashes). The old Library, Conversation and Presses
screens are cut. Nothing in the app needs an agent's session: what waits on him comes to the app, and a session's link is only ever a quiet link (his word, 20 Nov). Every screen reads through `ReadScreen` (loading, empty, stale, unreachable, failed, not paired) and every
action through `LifeStore`'s `ActionState`. Manrope is the one font (`DesignSystem/Typography.swift`). `Shared/Palette.swift` is the phone's one copy of Life's brand (`assets/brand/tokens.css`), checked by
`scripts/brand.test.ts`: no colour literal elsewhere, except the hardware drawing (`LightGuide.swift`), which paint the pendant itself, and a draft's chat strip, drawn in its platform's own colours (`ChatLook` in `DesignSystem/DraftBlock.swift`, a style table keyed by network, decision 82; what a chat can do comes from the draft's own fields, never from that table). `docs/architecture.md` names every store and owner and says where a new screen or derived value goes.

An agent is shown by its program's mark, never a star (`RuntimeName.mark`, `DesignSystem/AgentMark.swift`): Claude's and OpenAI's own, from their makers' kits, as custom symbols in `Lifelog/Marks.xcassets`, in one colour (OpenAI's terms allow none on its mark), a terminal for any other program; the program is the asking agent's, named by the ask's `agent` (`Check.askingRuntime`); an ask that names none (his own app's, a paired agent's) shows the terminal, never a guess. Every free-text field is a `DictationField` (`DesignSystem/DictationField.swift`): typing and the one microphone (a tap starts, pauses and goes on; a hold records while held), each run one `DictationCapture` whose words land at the caret at its pause, driven by Kit's `Dictation`; no screen draws a `TextField` of its own. A field with a single submit (Ask Life, a matter's goal or next step, the new agent's goal, Settings' vocabulary) submits through the field's `send:` (its Send, and a swipe up from the microphone), never a Save, Start or Add button of its own; a card's answers stay buttons. The spec's components live in `Lifelog/DesignSystem/Pieces.swift` and screens draw none of their own (a matter's steps are `DesignSystem/StepTimeline.swift`; badge, link, quiet chip, add row, busy pill, spinner (`Spinner`, never `ProgressView`), segment, paper box, row, row group, field, state mark); one radius per job (`Metrics.Radius`). A list is one grouped inset card per part (decision 98, `groupedRow`): rows at least `Metrics.listRow` (60) high on `card`, no divider, the open one lit with `pick`; a part's label is a `ListLabel`, its one action a chip at its right; settings groups stay `RowGroup`. Nothing you can press is bare text (decision 85): an action or what opens a sheet is a `QuietChip` (its glyph, on a `fillTint` capsule), going somewhere a `TextLink` in `fg` with its chevron or outward arrow, adding a line an `AddRow`; a context's Markdown links stay as they are. `scripts/design-system.test.ts` counts what screens still draw by hand; its caps only go down. A PR that changes a screen gets a design review before its debt verdict: a fresh agent of junior rank (sonnet, not haiku: judging two pictures needs more than the cheapest) with `design-review.md`, never the builder or the designer; it captures every state and transition itself, the designed stills with `bun run design:shots`. The same file runs as a whole-app audit after a redesign lands.

The fixture's content is the design data (`apps/lifelog/fixtures/design/`, one JSON file per connector read, wire shape), the one copy of it: the designer owns what it says and writes it, strings byte for byte as the frames show them, and their frame generator reads it from the root checkout; `LifeFixture` only reads it and keeps the behaviour (answered cards drop, scenarios, outcomes). The frame map has two halves joined by frame name: the designer's (`frame-map.json` in their folder: pose, size, picture, state) and ours (`frames.json` beside the data: launch arguments and taps, or why a frame can't be reached).

## How to

- **Regenerate the project** after editing `project.yml`: `bun run ios:project`.
- **Test Kit alone**, from Life's root: `swift test --package-path apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Kit --scratch-path .build/kit`.
- **Run in the simulator.** Debug builds take `-fixtureLife <scenario>` (Life without a Mac: `Fixtures/LifeFixture.swift`, reading the design data by its path on the Mac, or `-fixtureReads <folder>`, with its
  `-fixtureCard`, `-fixtureTab projects|people`, `-fixtureFilter moves`, `-fixtureShow matters`, `-fixtureMatter`, `-fixtureParked <project id>`, `-fixturePerson <ref>`, `-fixtureSheet pendant|settings|agent|agents|agentPage|icon|pictures|pair|pairing|replacePendant|pairPendant|create`, `-fixturePair ok|slow|expired`, `-fixturePage life|server|diagnostics|apps|logs|spending`, `-fixtureAnswer`, `-fixtureStart`, and `-fixturePose wide|upright`, the iPhone Duo open drawn on any simulator), `-fixtureLibrary` (a demo library with audio), `-fakePendant <scenario>`
  (see `FakeScenario.swift`) `-offline` and `-serverFlaky` (the server times out and every other request is called off); launch with `xcrun simctl launch <udid> com.ericedouard.lifelog <args>`.
  The device token goes in through `SIMCTL_CHILD_LIFELOG_DEVICE_TOKEN` with `SIMCTL_CHILD_LIFELOG_SERVER_URL`, read from the file without printing it.
- **Check Release before an install.** The gate builds Debug only and `install:phone` builds Release, so anything
  DEBUG-only that leaks out of `#if DEBUG` breaks the install:
  `xcodebuild -project apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Lifelog.xcodeproj -scheme Lifelog -configuration Release -destination 'generic/platform=iOS Simulator' build`.
- **Install on the phone** with `bun run install:phone -- --no-watch`; include the Watch only when the Watch app
  changed. If the Watch install fails with "Connection invalid", the owner restarts the Watch on its charger and you
  retry once.
- **Write SwiftUI** after reading Xcode's own `swiftui-whats-new-27` and `swiftui-specialist` skills, in
  `/Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/PlugIns/IDEIntelligenceChat.framework/Versions/A/Resources/`.

## Worth knowing

- **Ask Life** (`Lifelog/AskLife/`, Kit's `AskLife`): its tab in the bar (a tap leaves the tab on show; the system places it in every pose, no position code of ours) opens a full-screen cover; his words (the one microphone, `DictationField` with a Send) go through `LifeAPI.askMainSession` (`ask_main_session`), one call whose progress lines (`thinking`, `working`, `words`) `AskLife` draws and whose answer ends it; a call that did not reach Life leaves his words waiting under one key. The screen sends refs only (`AppRouter.mainSessionScreen`); `keepWarm` (no words) runs while the screen is open. An answer's `show` (Kit's `AskLifeShow`, once per answer: `AskLife.shown`) is applied by `AppRouter.show`: a page opens as a tap on it would, or a tab's list is cut to Life's rows (`AppRouter.cut`, one at a time: Projects on All and People in Life's order, the Inbox keeping what blocks first) under a toast first in the list, in the chips' place on Projects, that stays until its X (`CutToast`); a chip, or the tab tapped again, gives the whole list back.

- **iPhone Duo: layout branches on `horizontalSizeClass` only**, never device, screen size or orientation: compact is one
  pane, regular two (`CardArrangement` in the Inbox, the `NavigationSplitView` in Projects and People). One exception, his
  decision 93: People open upright (taller than wide) stacks as folded, the list full screen and a tap pushing the page
  (`PeopleList.isSplit`, the one place the pose decides, measured before People draws). State a fold must keep lives
  above the branch (`AppRouter`, the screen). Inside a split view's column read `windowSizeClass` (set at the root): the
  list column is compact even on the open Duo.
  The one other input is where the system puts the bars, its `toolbarVerticalEdge` (`reportsSideColumn`), never a guess: the
  Undo toast is a capsule in that column only on a compact width (the closed Duo); the open Duo keeps the worded toast, as designed.
- Every bundle id, the app group and the background task derive from `LIFELOG_BUNDLE_ID` in `project.yml` (beside
  `DEVELOPMENT_TEAM`); code reads them from the bundle's Info.plist (`AppGroup`, `SyncStore.backgroundTaskID`, `Keychain`). The Keychain
  service is `<app's bundle id>.server`, named from `LifelogBundleID` in every target that reads it (`Keychain`), which
  for the owner's build is the name it always had, so his stored token stays readable; changing the bundle id starts
  unpaired.
- The app is Life's Inbox and Projects, read through the connector, plus the Pendant's capture and upload. No screen
  reads the library; its cache of the server's feed stays, and any screen that returns reads the cache first
  (`docs/architecture.md`, "Local first").
- A source's `sentThrough` (on the server) means everything before it was sent or decided. A new install's uploader
  starts there (`Uploader.restore`) and never reads derived files below it: `derived/` and `upload/` are kept out of
  backups, `timeline.sqlite` is not, so a migrated phone has an index of sidecars it lacks. A lost sidecar is
  unanalyzed audio, never skipped (unlabelled audio counts as possible speech); from the cursor on it is rebuilt and
  uploads wait for it (`TimelineScanner.scan`).
- The owner's edits are `OwnerIntent`s sent with `SyncStore.perform`: queued in the outbox, shown at once. A value the
  server holds is never shown as known: asking, then the value (`serverValue`, `serverVocabulary`); a change shows at
  once, then Saved, or goes back with the reason. Settings draws only `Row` and `RowGroup`.
- Pairing goes one way: a `lifelog://pair` link (Camera or pasted) is opened by `RootView`, which shows `PairSheet`: it
  confirms naming each server, and only then `SyncStore.pair` or `LifeStore.pair` stores it. A server's token is checked
  against the server before it is kept (`Pairing.pair`), so a failed pairing leaves the old one working. A link never
  repoints the app alone, and a token is never drawn.
- `SyncStore` is the one driver: uploads, the outbox, the feed and the status. Debug builds pair from
  `LIFELOG_DEVICE_TOKEN` and `LIFELOG_SERVER_URL` in the environment (`DeviceToken.importFromEnvironment`), the one
  debug path.
- The app talks to Life only through its connector, in Kit's `LifeConnector`: its OAuth (pairing by the code in the
  `life` and `code` of the pairing link), one `MCPClient`, and `LifeAPI`, the only place tool names appear. Every read
  is a `ReadState` (`LifeStore` shows its `shown`), never a bare value, and runs one at a time per kind within a pairing with one more pass (`ReadFlights`), so an older read never lands last; a pass queued under an earlier pairing never begins, and one begun under it applies nothing (`PairingGeneration`). Out of reach (no network path, or the newest read found no way to Life: `LifeConnection`, `LifeStore.whyOutOfReach`) the app is read-only: `ReadScreen` says why above what it shows, every action control is off (`offWhileOutOfReach`), and `LifeStore` refuses a screen's tap before it holds or shows anything; a notification's answer, or one held before, is still tried. The one `NWPathMonitor` is `NetworkPath`, which the pendant's sync reads too; back on the network, every read screen reads again. The fixtures in `fixtures/wire/life/` are written
  by Life's server tests (`WRITE_WIRE=1 bun test apps/server/src/host/http/mcp-phone-reads.test.ts`, and the answer and
  agent tests through `wire-fixture.ts`).
- Every action on Life (an answer, a start, stop, resume, model, his edit of a matter) runs in `LifeActions` and shows
  as an `ActionState` in `LifeStore.actions` (`ActionBook`, one pairing's: a new pairing empties it, and a late event of
  an earlier one's action applies nothing; an answer's repeats go only while its pairing holds): running with the steps
  as they stream, succeeded with proof, failed with its step, reason and whether a retry is allowed. Only the server ends one; past its `limitSeconds` it is late, never
  failed. An answer carries one `life/answerKey` (made at his tap, kept while the same tap repeats after a dropped
  connection, new after any result Life gave or for another answer to the same card), so Life sends nothing twice: a repeat of an answered card's same answer gets its first result under any key. Every action ends with one `life.action` event (steps with their seconds, limit, late, outcome): read it with `bun run events life.action` to see where a slow one spent its time. A
  card's buttons are its options' `label`s, and what an option `takes` (a person, a name, a note) is what the tap asks
  for (`Check.answer`; a `takes` this build doesn't know opens "Tell the agent…", on every agent card, never a dead
  button); a card's `context` is Markdown behind one link worded by the code (`Check.contextLinkTitle`; none without a context), which opens `ContextSheet` (`DesignSystem/ContextSheet.swift`: the system sheet, fit, medium and large; its blocks read in Kit by `ContextMarkdown`, the one reader of Markdown; a `life:` link's page, `fetch`, pushed inside with Back; a web link in Safari's view), and the open Duo's `ContextRegion` draws the same `ContextView` in place; a card's `needs` are drawn by `NeedsBlock` from `Check.shownNeedRows` (decided in Kit; a kind it doesn't know is
  its `line`; a link shows its full host and opens only on his tap), a QR with its text as the card's hero (`PayCodeBlock`, `Check.payCode`, drawn by Core Image), and a transfer's account `scheme` makes the filled answer his bank app (`Check.transfers`, `BankApp`: his pick per kind on Settings > Apps, kept on the phone; a QR is saved to Photos first, add-only), every option quiet beside it, and Life's `doneSeen` is one line above the
  answers when seen or not found (`DoneSeenLine`); while Life is looking, one busy answer stands in their place; the phone decides nothing Life decides (what is open, a goal's steps): a matter's edits and Mark
  as done go by its id. A card's push is rewritten with the card's words by the notification extension (`NotificationService/`, `CardNotification`), which reads Life at the pairing's origin and no other and stamps the card with that origin (`CardNotification.read`); a draft's card takes the send category, so Send shows on the Watch too (`CardNotification.sendCategory`; not while his Face ID setting is on, `AppGroup.asksFaceIDToSend`, which only the phone can ask); expanded, it shows the draft as its chat strip (`DraftBlock`), the card's one-tap answers and Send (`NotificationContent/`, `CardNotification.isAnswerAllowed`), which the app answers in the background through `LifeStore.answer` (`IncidentNotifier`), on the card as the Life paired now has it (`LifeStore.notifiedCard`, `CardNotification.card`: the kept card only under the pairing it was read from, else Life's read of its `pushId`), never the kept card as it is; every answer goes to the Life it was decided for (`PairedLife`, captured at the tap, a row's when its answer was sent): each try and retry at that origin with that Life's bearer (`MCPClient.call(at:)`), and none once another pairing began; a tapped push routes by `pushId` with the card the extension kept (`NotificationRoute.card`) and opens the Inbox on that card at once, from the cached inbox or the kept card, while one whole read checks it (`PushedCard`, the pager's spinner); with neither, the card-shaped skeleton waits for Life's read of its `pushId`, beside the whole read; a push while
  the app is in front reads the inbox again. The badge is `Inbox.badge`: the asks that block him (`urgency`).
- **A bulk card** (`Inbox/BulkCard.swift`, the batch close's look): a kind with 2 or more asks Life pre-ticks (`Check.preTicked`, the server's rule; the phone never decides it) folds into its first ask in the pager and the list (`InboxOrder.folded`) until he goes one by one (`CardNotes.unfolded`). A row's body opens its own card in a sheet.   the app is in front reads the inbox again. An appointment Life added to his calendar (`calendar.added`, category
  `life.calendar`) is written the same way from `read_appointments` (`CalendarNotification`); its Remove runs in the
  background through `LifeStore.changeAppointment` and replaces the notification with how it went, Open in Calendar
  opens Calendar on its day (`calshow:`; Calendar opens no other app's event), and a tap opens its event sheet
  (`AppointmentSheet`, `AppRouter.appointment`) over the tab he was on. The badge is `Inbox.badge`: the asks that block him (`urgency`).
- **A bulk card** (`Inbox/BulkCard.swift`, the batch close's look): a kind with 2 or more asks Life pre-ticks (`Check.preTicked`, the server's rule; the phone never decides it) folds into its first ask in the pager and the list (`InboxOrder.folded`) until he goes one by one (`CardNotes.unfolded`). "Answer N" sends one `answer_check` per ticked row at once (`LifeStore.answerRows`): the rows stay, working, until Life has answered them all, then its word in the toast and on to the next ask; rows Life refused come back alone, ticked, with its reason above the answers. A row's body opens its own card in a sheet.
- Live logs (Settings, Help: `Settings/LiveLogsPage.swift`) is the one read that is never cached and never in `LifeStore`: the page owns its feed (`LogFeed`, Kit), one poller every 2 s while it shows, reading only what the log gained after its `head` (so a quiet poll is one read), at most 500 lines (shown and paused together; older ones read again from under the oldest kept line, whose `id` is that cursor), and a read begun for another filter or pairing applies nothing. Settings' row counts the last hour's problems from one page (`problemsLastHour`, said only when known); a feed read back past 500 lines drops its newest (`LogFeed.isCut`), and following again starts it over.
- Spending (Settings' own row, which opens it in the Projects tab's detail column with the tabs, `AppRouter.show(.spending)`: `Settings/SpendingPage.swift`) reads `read_spending` through `LifeStore` like any read; Kit's `ModelSpending` decides its rows (`sections`: billed dollars and his plans' tokens never added, the Mac's models one row at $0) and `SpendWords` every figure. A top matter opens its page, a top agent its page (`AppRouter.open(agent:)`). An agent's page and a matter's page end with one quiet line of what their agents spent (`spendingLine`), absent when the desk can't be read. Inside Settings, a read's "Pair with Life" opens Settings' Life page (`ReadScreen.pair`); on Spending, Settings.
- A server enum decodes unknown values as `unknown` (`UnknownTolerant`) and belongs in the mirror list of
  `ContractTests.swift` (lifelog's against its `openapi.json` in `mirroredEnumsMatchTheSpec`, Life's against `apps/server/src` in `lifesEnumsAreTheServers`), which also checks
  every path the client calls.
- The top group is one `ToolbarItem` per control, never a `ToolbarItemGroup`: the closed Duo's side column took a group's first item only and left the gear in a bar of its own over the screen's title. On a pushed page in that column Back is ours, declared after the Pendant and gear (Pendant, gear, Back, then the tabs).
- The toolbar API is `toolbarMinimizationBehavior(_:for:)`; the WWDC docs' `toolbarMinimizeBehavior` doesn't compile.
- A read that failed after one landed, or Life out of reach, is said by the designed `Toast` over the pane (`StaleNote`, `staleToast`), Retry in its capsule; never a band in the layout. A matter's agent spins only while its turn runs (`AgentStatus.running`); waiting, it is its program's mark, still. A call (`tel:`, FaceTime) asks first; a chat opens at once. Back from a person opened from elsewhere returns to the tab he came from (`AppRouter.closePerson`).
- An Omi drains through the same `DrainCoordinator` as the Limitless Pendant, by its `DeviceCodec`. **Any work on the
  Omi starts by reading the latest Omi firmware release notes for the ring-custody fix** (`docs/research/omi.md`,
  first section). Once it ships, set `OmiFirmware.custodyFixedIn`, which turns the in-app warning into an update
  prompt, and say so to the owner in your report. Pairing another model moves `CaptureStore.deviceID` to its slot, so
  the first model's pages not uploaded yet stop uploading: Replace (`CaptureStore.replacePendant`) refuses until
  `whatIsNotOnTheMac` is empty, and a lost pendant is replaced only after the cost line and an explicit confirm.
  `.forget` clears only the phone's remembered pendant, never the pendant's own pairing or its audio.
- A widget's `configurationDisplayName` and `description` take a literal or a `String`: an interpolated literal is
  formatted text, and WidgetKit traps on it when the extension launches.
- Never write `<=` or `>=` between two `Date`s in the phone's Swift: write `!(a < b)`. macOS 27.2 beta lacks those
  two operators, so a test suite that uses one dies at launch (`Symbol not found … Date.<=`, or signal 11) and the
  gate goes red. That is this rule broken, never an OS bug to push past. Drop the rule once
  `print(Date() <= Date())` runs as a Swift program on the Mac.
