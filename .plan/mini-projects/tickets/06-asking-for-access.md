# Asking for access

## Question

How does Life ask for what only the user can grant (a permission, a setting, a source turned on): one bundled ask, its card, how Life sees each step done without asking again, and what it does when a nice-to-have is refused.

## Answer

Decided with the owner on 28 Nov 2026. macOS shows permission prompts only on the Mac, in a logged-in session, and Full
Disk Access and Accessibility only in System Settings (What an agent can reach on the Mac); the user usually talks to
Life from the phone.

- **Phone card, Mac on cue:** the ask is a card on the phone saying what is needed and why, with "I'm at my Mac". Its
  tap makes Life fire the prompt, or open the right System Settings pane, on the Mac. Nothing waits unseen on the Mac.
- **One card, self-ticking steps:** all that is missing in one card, numbered plain steps, each ticked as Life sees it
  done through the silent checks; the user never says "done". Steps that are not permissions (turning on "Download
  originals" in iCloud Photos, freeing disk space) are steps of the same card.
- **Refused or ignored:** a refused nice-to-have: Life goes on without it and says what is lost. A refused must-have: it
  says plainly it can't go on that way and offers another way, or dropping the project. Ignored: the card stays in the
  inbox, never chased (an open step stays asked).
