# Life suggests

## Question

The agent should work out by itself what Life makes possible and suggest it (personal invites with each friend's picture, the contacts to invite, being told when someone opens the link), and suggest follow-up projects (after the party, a photo dump all guests can add to, made into an album). How and when it suggests, without adding load.

## Answer

Decided with the owner on 28 Nov 2026. The agent works out what Life makes possible and suggests it inside the
questions (pre-ticked choices) and the preview; follow-up projects (a photo dump after the party) come as one card on
the done page. Never as separate cards while it works.
