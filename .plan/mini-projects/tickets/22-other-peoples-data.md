# Other people's data in a project

## Question

Guests' answers, the pictures and videos they upload: where they are kept, who sees them, what the public page shows, and how they are deleted.

## Answer

Decided with the owner on 28 Nov 2026. Guests' answers and uploads are kept with the project, like its other files; the
public page shows only what the approved preview shows.
