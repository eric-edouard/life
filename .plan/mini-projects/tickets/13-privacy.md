# Privacy

## Question

What leaves the Mac during a project (an agent looking at photos itself sends them to the model's hosts; Life's own photo descriptions stay on the Mac), what the user must be told, and what changes in `SECURITY.md`.

## Answer

Decided with the owner on 28 Nov 2026. The access card says in one plain line what the agent will read and that it goes
to Life's model; the agent always asks for the narrowest grant that does the job, never Full Disk Access when a folder
or Photos access will do (a grant covers every agent's script: What an agent can reach on the Mac). `SECURITY.md`
gains the project agents' reads and the known limit of Doing the work safely.
