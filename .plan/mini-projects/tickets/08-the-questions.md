# The questions about what they want

## Question

How does the agent ask what the user means, grounded in what it saw in their data, all in one go: the format (a card, options, an artifact), how many questions at most, and what it does with no answer.

## Answer

Decided with the owner on 28 Nov 2026 (general: a mini project is any result to shape, a site, a trip plan, a budget,
a CV, a move of notes, never only photos).

- **One card**: each question with choices built from what the agent saw in their data, Life's recommendation first, a
  free answer always open.
- **At most five**, only what changes the result; Life decides the rest itself and shows those choices in the preview.
- **Real examples from their data** shown in the question, in their own app.
- **No answer after a day:** Life builds the preview on its recommended answers and says so. A preview touches nothing
  of theirs.
