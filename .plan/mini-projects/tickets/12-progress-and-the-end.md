# Progress and the end

## Question

Where a running project shows in the user's app and how it shows without adding load; how it ends: done with a report, "not possible, here's why", paused or dropped by the user; and what happens to its agent and its record.

## Answer

Decided with the owner on 28 Nov 2026.

- **Progress:** the project's one quiet line (Answering later).
- **Done:** a short done page: what was done, before and after, where the result is, how to undo, what it cost; a push
  links it. The project's status becomes `done`.
- **Not possible or dropped:** status `dropped`, its reason in the project's body, said plainly to the user.
- **After:** the agent ends; its folder and git are kept. A later "change X" about it opens a new agent on the project,
  reading that folder.
