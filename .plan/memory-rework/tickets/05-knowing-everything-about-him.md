# What knowing everything about him means

## Question

Which plain facts about him and his world must Life know at any age (his biography, family, places lived, work history, the people in his life and who they are, what happened when), where each lives, and how an old fact buried in a 2019 message reaches the memory without the middle layers.

## Decided by the designer (10 Nov; spec sections 4, 7, 8)

- The facts of his life at any age (family, places lived, work, the people in his life, what happened when) are
  lasting claims in the evidence log. The lasting families of read (acts, mentions, self-reports, lasting claims,
  promises with their endings) run at every age; only the practical claims are read near now alone. So a fact in a
  2019 message reaches the memory through the evidence read, with no middle layer.
- They are carried up by rule into his history at every level, and into Life's list where code computes with them.
- A hole that matters becomes a question asked in conversation.
- His answers to his project loops (`decisions.jsonl`) are his word.
