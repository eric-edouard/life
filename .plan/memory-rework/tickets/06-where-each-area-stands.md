# Where each area stands

## Question

For each area and project of his life: what its state is (moving, stuck, drifting, done), what evidence decides it, how fresh it must be, and how Life says it directly. One state per area, owned by one step.

## Decided by the designer (10 Nov; spec sections 6, 8)

- Every part of his life is a subject with a history and a standing, by the same mechanism as a person. One standing
  per subject, owned by one step.
- A part of his life he never declared is named by Life, used at once, corrected by his word.
- The standing carries a word set by code from the evidence (moving, waiting on someone, stuck, drifting, dormant,
  done) and one or two lines read by a model: where it stands, what moved, what blocks it.
- Fresh by construction: the last summary plus the evidence since.
