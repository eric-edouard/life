# Closeness by depth

## Question

What makes closeness, if not volume or time: sharing, support, warmth, personal vs work, years known, his word. How it is read, what it gates (pages, situations, the overview), and how it never decides who is read.

What he confides to someone about third parties and his own life (talk about absent people) is a depth signal for that listener.
