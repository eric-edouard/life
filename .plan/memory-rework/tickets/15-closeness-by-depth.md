# Closeness by depth

## Question

What makes closeness, if not volume or time: sharing, support, warmth, personal vs work, years known, his word. How it is read, what it gates (pages, situations, the overview), and how it never decides who is read.

What he confides to someone about third parties and his own life (talk about absent people) is a depth signal for that listener.

## His word (10 Nov, during the exchange grilling)

"People I interact with a lot != closeness." How often or how much he talks with someone never makes them close. The
close read covering every two-sided exchange is coverage, not closeness; the measured facts per exchange (who started,
reply delays, shares) are evidence for meters, never a closeness score by themselves.

## Decided by the designer (10 Nov; spec section 9)

- Closeness is depth, counted: what is confided each way, support exchanged, the personal against the practical,
  years known; personal and work apart; his word above it.
- It gates nothing: everyone has a history as deep as their evidence. It ranks who shows in the overview and in a
  call's context.
