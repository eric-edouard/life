# Him as the observed subject

## Question

The objective observer: his own persona and meters (the same as everyone's), what repeats in how he acts across people and years, the gaps between what he says and what he does, his psych state over time from his own words. What evidence earns a "meta" line, so it is observed and never a guess.

From issue #390: how he felt over time (today the episode read forbids feelings and his mood is rewritten nightly), his routines, and what he resolves set against what he then does.

## Decided so far (10 Nov)

- Life tells him nothing itself (its nightly mail aside): it writes its reading into the memory, and an agent in a
  chat or his daily and weekly mails read it to him. Raising a thing unasked stays the agents' job.
- When his actions contradict his words, Life writes the gap and what it means, plainly, with the evidence under it
  (his answer): "first goal: 4% of his time this month; by his actions it is not his first goal". One conclusion,
  written once, so two agents never tell him different things.
