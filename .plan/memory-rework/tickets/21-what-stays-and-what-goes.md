# What stays and what goes

## Question

Among today's layers (units, episodes, facts, pages, situations, personas, closeness, communication styles, the index): which stay, which change, which go, so the new design is leaner than today while every feature on the checklist stays.

- Units, episodes, observations, open items, appointments, search and the connector stay. Pages become a view.
  Situations, personas and closeness are replaced by the standing, the counted readings and the story. `truth/`
  becomes Life's list and his word. Every feature of ticket 01 stays.
