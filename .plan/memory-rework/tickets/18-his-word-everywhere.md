# His word in every layer

## Question

How his corrections, ratings, labels, told lines and `truth/` enter each layer, and why they survive any re-read (an input to every build rather than an annotation on an output). His facts beat a reading; his views of people are shown as his view.

## Proposed by the designer (10 Nov), waiting on his yes

His question: what is `truth/`, how does he make something a truth, is it needed? Answer: not as a thing he keeps.
His word is what he tells Life (a chat with an agent, "hey Life", a correction, a fact-check answer): said once, it
holds. Life keeps its own list of subjects (people, parts of his life, goals and their order), used at once and
corrected by his word. What he says to other people is evidence about him, never his word. What `truth/` holds today
stays as his word. This changes invariants of `AGENTS.md` ("only the owner writes `truth/`", "his goals live there
only", "a new key in `truth/` lands in code first"), so it needs his yes. Detail: architecture.md, "His word, and
Life's own list".
