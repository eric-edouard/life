# The meters

## Question

Which trait and relationship meters, with poles in his words; how each is counted from codes (rates per exposure, the strongest level kept, never pulled to the middle), its vantage (with him, with others, reported), the "too few to say" gate, lately vs before, and how the meters reach agents as data.

Evidence told by him or others about a person sits in its own vantage ("as he tells it to A"), never mixed with the person's own conduct.

His word again (10 Nov): he has a different persona with each person (with a parent he plays a role; what he tells
others is closer to what he thinks), and group conversations feed each person's persona. In the architecture: every
act names who did it, to whom and in front of whom, so every meter can be cut by counterpart; his conduct with
someone and his view of them are two readings, never mixed, and their difference is kept.

## Decided by the designer (10 Nov; spec section 9)

- A meter is a rate per chance to act, in one vantage, shown as a band with its count; "too few to say" below a
  minimum; no pull toward a typical person; how far at its strongest kept beside how often; lately against before.
- Fifteen about a person, twelve between him and a person, listed in the spec. The poles' words need his yes.
- They reach agents as data in the subject read.

His questions (10 Nov): is the spec grounded in the psychology research, can he draw a trait profile (Big Five), can
he analyse his dynamics with people. Spec section 9 now names the accepted construct behind every meter (the Big
Five's ten aspects, Honesty-Humility, attachment as behaviour only; the twelve dyad meters of research 02), says how
well text carries each, keeps the word persona for the view, keeps how each writes, and states the two places it
departs from the research by his decisions (no shrinking; no exchanges labelled by him). Open: one research note on
impulsivity before the pilot.
