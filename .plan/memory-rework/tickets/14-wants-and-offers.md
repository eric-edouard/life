# Wants and offers

## Question

The interpersonal market in four directions (what they want, in general and from him; what they can offer, in general and to him; what he wants from them; what he can offer them): the constructs, what counts as evidence (given, asked, said), and how an inferred want is shown as inferred.

## Decided by the designer (10 Nov; spec sections 5, 9)

- From the giving-and-asking read (asks, offers, gives, refuses, returns, by six kinds of resource) and from what
  people say they want. The four directions are cuts of the same records. Shown as asked, given, said; a want nobody
  stated is written only in the story, marked inferred.
