# The questions he asks Life

## Question

Collect the questions he has actually asked Life or an agent about his life (his ChatGPT and Claude sessions through the connector, his asks, voice asks, the problem doc, the 8 Nov email), and sort them by area and kind: where things stand, practical, relationship, period, prediction, about himself. Keep their words (in his data repo, never here). They become the test set every later decision is judged against, so note for each which answer would count as right.

## Answer

The set lives in his data repo (personal): `docs/memory-rework/02-questions.md`, 102 questions in his words with date,
source and what a right answer contains, sorted into where things stand, practical, relationship, period or arc,
prediction, and about himself; 25 marked failed from his own record.

- What he actually asks is mostly practical and where things stand (from his asks since 19 Oct and his Claude sessions).
  Practical failures are open loops no step owned; relationship and arc failures are his 8–9 Nov corrections.
- The kinds the destination cares most about are thin: prediction has no real past question (only his 9 Nov
  requirements), period or arc has three real asks, about himself about five. No test reaches before Sep 2026.
- The connector's call log holds agents' keyword searches, not his questions; his ChatGPT and claude.ai conversations
  are not on disk yet (exports requested 9 Nov).
- So the set grows from his old chats and assistant exports, once in (his ask of 9 Nov: the personal moments that
  matter, code and work chatter filtered out), before Validation uses it. The 18 existing eval questions were graded by
  a model, not by him.
- Extended the same day (his ask): 196 more questions mined from his chats since 2015, about 36 of each thin kind,
  predictions as backtests on what actually followed; quotes checked verbatim. Old WhatsApp and his assistant chats
  follow when the iPhone backup and the exports land.
