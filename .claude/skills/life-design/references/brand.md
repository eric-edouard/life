## The rules in force (philosophy.md, "The look", "Buttons and colour", "Words")

- Colours: the 13 tokens plus `tint`, `glass`, `wash`. `flag` needs him, `ai` (blue) is Life's guess, `ok` it worked;
  the exceptions are an app's colours on its drafts, faces' hues and the aura.
- Cream (`fg` fill) marks the main answer, a chosen value, the live microphone, Send and "Delete for both".
- Manrope, seven sizes (the `--text-*` tokens), weights 450, 600, 650; no capitals.
- Radii: pill, 12, 18, 22. Spacing 8, 16, 24, 48. No borders, outlines, rings or hairlines; the draft's dash and the
  edge are the exceptions.
- Dark only on the phone, for now. Emoji are monotone.
- Words only on answers, a confirm's two choices and a draft's quick-edit chips, three at most; "Later" is the one quiet
  word. Life may say "I"; a button never does. The interface says "you".
