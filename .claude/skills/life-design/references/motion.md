## The rules in force

- Every state change, new element and layout shift animates; a thing that changes state stays the same thing.
- What moves by itself: the edge (when on), the pendant's dot, the ink-in of words, the suggested asks and the
  thinking shimmer. A long task is a still ring that fills at each milestone, never a spinner.
- Words ink in from blurred to sharp; his own words appear at once; under Reduce Motion everything appears whole.
- Each step line replaces the last: the old blurs away upward, the new inks in.
- Unfolding measures first, scrolls just enough, then opens in one 0.4 s reveal; folding reverses it.
- The edge glow is off by default in the app, on by his setting; designs keep it. State changes on it glide over
  about a second.
- Motion is dialled back only after he has used it on the phone.
