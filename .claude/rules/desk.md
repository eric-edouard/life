# The desk, registered agents and wakes

Moved from the root `AGENTS.md` ("Invariants" and "Gotchas"). `SECURITY.md`, "Agents", is the security reading of the same rules.

- **Content never runs Life's code.** Recorded text never makes Life's own code act; model calls see no tools and no
  instruction files (`apps/server/src/models/llm.ts`). The one call given tools is the question cases' agent
  (`scripts/question-cases.ts`, his word of 10 Nov 2026): Codex, with Life's connector serving its reads only, and
  nothing else. An agent reads what it pulls through the connector as data
  (`SECURITY.md`, "Agents"). The desk wakes an agent on the events it waits for, one assignment of each agent per event (`routeOf`), and a wake hands the event's facts (who, where,
  when; for a payment: its amount, in EUR too, its currency, whether it is spending, the money app's category, and when; for an arrival at a place: when, and where (its coordinates); for a GitHub activity: its repo, number, who, kind (`issue.closed`, `pr.merged`, `comment`, `review`) and link), never its words; on a matter it also opens with Life's own record of that matter (`matterRecordOf`: its derived steps, goal and next step, and the assignment's notes), which is Life's reading, never the event's text (his word of 20 Nov 2026: nothing may rest on the agent's will or memory). Words choose a wake in three ways only: the agent's own pattern on the text of a message as its readers see it (its files' lines included: a voice note's words, a photo's description), or on a payment's merchant, category and note, or on an arrival's line (`arrived <place>`), or on a GitHub activity's title and body as GitHub gives them
  (`text_matches` in its wait, his word of 20 Nov 2026: "agents that wake on events they waited on rather
  than timers"; for waits on every source, his word of 20 Nov 2026: "waits could be on all sources"; for GitHub, his direction of 20 Nov 2026, life#774: a wait names a repo, `owner/name` or `owner/*`, on `github_activity`, and only the repos the open waits name are read, through `gh` with conditional requests, never stored; the connector's poll and the wait share one filter and one matcher, `EventFilter` and
  `matcherOf`), and closeness in meaning to its matter (the wide net, `desk/matter-net.ts`, his word of 18 Nov
  2026: anything new close to its matter, by the Mac's embedder), and the arrival reader's confirmed answer that new
  lines hold an ask, a promise or a wait for him, or his own word that he will do something (his yes of 20 Nov 2026: "Yes, build it"; `memory/arrival-reader.ts`,
  `confirmedArrivalsOf`): Apple's on-device yes alone never wakes, only his engine's confirm (what is still left for him, by its priority: a low one wakes no one and notifies nothing, his decision of 21 Nov 2026; for now it is only kept for the night's facts, its quiet place in his inbox not built yet), and it wakes one
  agent, chosen once in code (`matterOrPersonalOf`, `desk/event-queue.ts`: a matter's agent by
  the people who wrote its lines, else the Personal agent, the desk registering it when none stands; an agent whose wait matched the message already had its
  `message.received`, and a message queued for any agent is never queued again; none once he wrote in its chat after another person's lines), with `arrival.confirmed`'s facts only; his spoken ask ("hey Life") wakes no agent: it is one ask of the main session, as his words through Ask Life are (his decision of 27 Nov 2026: "The main session"; `desk/spoken-ask-intake.ts`), which hands work on with `tell_agent` (its `ask.spoken` event, facts only: where his words are, never them). In all three ways, Life's code only tests the text and
  queues the wake: the text is never run, never taken as an instruction, never handed over; the agent reads it
  itself, as data. His spoken ask is not one of them: heard as his (`wakeOf` high, `host/hear.ts`), its words are the
  main session's prompt, as his Ask Life words are. A call that ended is such an event (`call.ended`, his word of 19 Nov 2026): queued once lifelog's own signals say its words are in, it hands the call's chat, span and people and its recording's id and turn range, never a word; what
  the agent then reads is the agent's to judge, outside Life's boundary (`SECURITY.md`, "Agents"). A mark (his words to Life, to keep) only keeps a unit read. His Apple Notes and imported
  assistant chats are memory only, never his words for actions. One fact of a message acts, never its words: that he
  wrote in an open draft card's chat since it was asked withdraws that card (`withdrawDraftsAnsweredInChat`, the
  watcher's 30 s step, life#731; its asker hears `decision.withdrawn`).

- **Life starts and wakes his registered agents, and nothing else of Life does** (his yes to the desk, 18 Nov 2026). The
  desk (`apps/server/src/desk/`, off until his `desk` setting) creates an agent only on his word: a project's (a one-off project's, once, when it is written with its goal: `openOneOffProject`, the owner's decision of 28 Nov 2026), or the
  Personal agent (its `role`, one active or stopped at most, its home `<agentsRepo>/personal/`), and wakes its session on its events, his answer or
  its deadline; the main session and the caretaker stand from the desk's start (his word of 22 Nov 2026: "we should
  create a main agent, and then we should also create the caretaker so that we don't run into issues later on"): each
  tick registers whichever has none active or stopped (`standUp`), with no assignment, so it runs no turn until his
  words or his complaint open one, never again after its last one never began until his next words or complaint (they
  take two of the desk's 50 active places); and on a confirmed arrival no matter's agent takes, or his words told to an agent that ended, with no
  Personal agent active or stopped, the desk registers it (Direction's ruling of 21 Nov 2026, under his word that every
  ask reaches an agent): one at most, never a second (`desk/role-agents.ts`, the one start of a standing role). An intention he gave through the main session (an `intention` instruction) opens one assignment on a standing agent, once, anchored on it, closed when it is replaced or its `until` passes; it starts no agent (`openIntentions`, `desk/hand-out-tick.ts`). A matter is never an agent of its own: it is an assignment of the agent that takes it (`agentForOf`: the
  oldest active agent of the project it shows under, his `matter-project` word first, else the Personal agent; several
  agents on one project is fine), given on his Start or by the hand-out, within his pace setting `desk.handOut`, on a
  running matter from the top of his ranking that involves someone he knows or holds a step he said himself
  (`isOneOfHis`) (his word of 20 Nov 2026: agents start on their own on his matters; nothing leaves without his yes):
  code picks the matter from his ranking (`handOutOf`, `desk/hand-out.ts`) and its agent, the agent reads the matter as
  data, and the card gate below is unchanged. Every matter sits with the agent that takes it (the fold, his word of 20
  Nov 2026): an open matter assignment another agent holds moves to it, with its wait, notes and cards, once its agent
  has no turn running, and an agent left with nothing open ends ("folded into the <name> agent"). With the hand-out
  on, a new matter of his own gets no Start card (the hand-out takes it); one that is not his waits for his Start. A
  project's agent holds only what is assigned to it. A matter he moved (`hand_over`, decision 78) stays with its new agent: the fold
  leaves it (`pinnedAssignmentsOf`); its old agent gets one notes turn in its own session (best effort, 10 minutes; the
  move commits without it), and a new holder whose first turn does not begin within 30 minutes, or ends unbegun, gets
  the move rolled back (the one exception to a first turn's not-started close), ending only an agent made for it; the
  new holder's first card is the open card it inherited, else its plan (`hasAsked` from the move). An agent changes a
  project's repo only on a card he approved, never writes its notes there, and never starts a session of its own to track a matter (his rulings of 20 Nov
  2026: the desk is the only dispatcher); a matter is its agent's to draft on (the Personal agent may draft to a
  project's work contacts until that project has its own agent). The night, the watcher and the connector never start one, and nothing recorded starts one directly: recorded content only makes a matter and its rank, and the hand-out's code picks from that. Life gives an agent a brief, the door and the send guard, and nothing else (on pi, also what Claude Code gives his agents there: the connector's reads over stdio, `life connector-reads`, subagents inside the guard, `pi-subagent.ts`, and `web_search`, Life's web search, `web-search/`): what an agent may do
  is its runtime's permissions and his own rules, never Life's code but the send guard (what the desk sets: an agent of his own Life is held back by nothing but sending and writing as him through his Life connector (his decisions of 19 and 20 Nov 2026; the direction session's ruling under his word of 20 Nov 2026): its session is one of his own in full (his settings, instruction files, skills, connectors, and his login shell's environment, `readLoginEnv`, less the runtime's own engine keys), in the runtime's no-ask mode set at every start and resume (`Row.mode`; Codex's at spawn), and every permission ask that still arrives is approved, with one exception: Life's send guard (`desk/send-guard.ts`), a hook of the session's own flags run before every tool call (in pi, Life's extension in its process, `pi-extension.ts`), refuses a send outside a card's draft and his connector's writes (`CONNECTOR_WRITES`) on any server but its `desk` door (SECURITY.md lists what, and what gets past it), Codex's hook trusted by the hash his own Codex gives (`readCodexGuardTrust`), and no agent starts without it; and a second Life's agent (a demo, `isOwnLife` false), whose runtime as he set it up would reach his own Life, gets its door alone: none of his settings, MCP servers, built-in tools, instruction files or memory, only its door's asks approved (`Row.onlyDoorPrefix` and a bare tool name), every other refused, none of his environment, never in his Claude or Codex app, in its runtime row, `runtime-rows.ts`; Codex's in Life's own Codex home). The brief carries his one
  gate (nothing outward in his name without his yes on the exact thing, asked as a decision; the Mac and the web are
  free) and no plan card (his yes of 25 Nov 2026, question 19): an agent starts work without asking and asks only what is his (anything outward, anything that spends, anything only he can do), each on the exact thing, and may `finish` or `set_wait` with no card (`desk/first-turns.ts`); a handed-out assignment that neither asked nor waits within two hours closes (`isCardOverdue`); a turn holding the main session's question (`main_question`, `desk/agent-questions.ts`) ends only once it read his words. The caretaker's plan is his complaint's words, and every outward act still goes through a card on the exact content (his yes of 20 Nov 2026); it is in the end user's hands, so it says nothing technical and fixes Life's memory itself, through its door's `correct_memory`, its `said` found in the words of a complaint at work on its assignment (`complaintWordsIn`); what lies in Life's code it audits from Life's public source, anonymized, and offers as a card whose mail draft goes to Life's developer (`DEVELOPER_CONTACT`), sent only on his Send (his rulings of 22 Nov 2026: "the caretaker is in the hands of the end user, so it shouldn't say anything technical. It should fix things itself"; "Bug reports should be an email to me"). The main session (role `main`, one at most, `desk/main-session.ts`, his decisions of 21 Nov 2026) is woken only by his words, through Ask Life or spoken ("hey Life"; words heard with low confidence run no turn), never by the clock, an event or the hand-out: each ask is one turn (`main_ask`, ended `replied`) on one session its runtime keeps open across asks (`Runtime.talk`); his words are its prompt as he sent them, with his screen's refs. It is one of his own sessions in full and acts with full power on his spoken or typed word (his yes of 21 Nov 2026): on a turn of his words its door gives it `request_decision` (a card asked as his own, `owner`), `change_registered_agent` (start, stop, resume, re-point, rename, change model, `hand_over`: the connector's writer, never on itself), `tell_agent` (his words to one agent, a spoken ask `to` its assignment, never routed by meaning, with the kind of work handed, `handed_work`; or a question its agent answers within 60 s, `askAssignment`), `read_assignment` (an assignment's record, read as data), `record_instruction` (his instruction, `recordInstruction`), `correct_memory` (a fact, or a matter done, its direction, importance, project or icon: `correctMatter`, `endByHisWord`), the connector's own writers for his to-do list, his calendar's appointments, what he tells Life and his directory (`change_todo_item`, `change_appointment`, `tell_life`, `change_directory_entry`; his rulings of 22 Nov 2026: "Ask Life should have everything"), `complain` (his complaint for the caretaker, through the one complaint record), and nothing else of the door; its answer is its Markdown, whose blocks (Life's components, `records/answer-blocks.ts`) are each checked when their fence closes (their refs open, their written values are in their `from`) and dropped when not; no other agent has these, but the caretaker's `correct_memory` (above). Each act takes `said`, which code finds in his words of that ask (`hisWordsIn`) and records as the act's words: it proves the words are his, never that the act matches them. The send guard is unchanged (its sends and his connector's writes refused), so nothing outward in his name goes without his yes on the exact thing, a card he taps. No agent that is not registered is started by
  Life; only the question cases' headless agent, by a developer's script (his word, 10 Nov 2026). A registered agent's state (its assignments, each with its subject, its wait and its notes file; its sessions, its
  turns) lives in the desk's store only (`desk.sqlite`), never in `memory/`; an assignment's notes are a file in his agents repo (`desk.agentsRepo`), the store holding its path. An assignment whose first turn never began (or not within its runtime's begin limit, its run then stopped) is closed, why in `why_not_started`, never retried (one cut by a restart of the desk helper is not: closed `cut` at its next start, its wake due again, `endTurnsCutByRestart`); an agent ends when its last open assignment closes. On the connector an agent is known by its
  pairing (its grant's agent claim); at the desk's door a registered agent is known by the key the desk gave its
  session; never by what it says. Nothing in Life branches on an agent's name.

- **Every spoken ask ends with something he sees** (his "Yes, always reply" of 27 Nov 2026): its thread entry with his words and Life's eyes, one push that Life heard him (its outcome replaces it: the same `pushId`, no words), then the main session's answer or a line saying what Life couldn't do and why; one said over an hour before (`SPOKEN_ASK_FRESH`), or that an agent received before, never runs (`askMainSession`; `spoken-asks.test.ts` guards it).

  app keeps one while the session is open, between his turns too): the wake stays queued until it lets go (`isSessionOpen`,
  `runtime-rows.ts`; a send it refuses deletes its unbegun turn), logged once per hold (`desk.session_open`) and shown on the
  agent (`sessionOpenIn`, amber). One session, one process. Every fresh Claude session, the main session's too, is listed in
  his Claude app's sidebar once, in the background, after Life's process lets it go (`listInApp`, `runtime-rows.ts`; his
  ask of 26 Nov 2026: "I still want all of the agents to appear in the sidebar"), never per turn: the import starts no
  process, only his typing there does (the 22 Nov freeze held wakes before any take-back). In front, and Terminal for pi,
  only on his "Continue on your Mac" (`continue_on_mac`, `desk/continue-on-mac.ts`); pi there loads Life's extension, the
  Claude app the Life plugin, so the send guard holds, and the plugin turns his app's asks into allows in an agent's

  the permissions mode"). A holder quiet 10 minutes when a wake is due (for the main session, with no wake needed) is ended by

- The watcher hears a conversation only once its speech is complete, by lifelog's own signals (`endedOnSilence`,
  `closed`), never a timing rule of Life's (`docs/operations.md`). The desk takes a call's words as in the same way (`recordingsOf`, `sources/lifelog-speech.ts`): a turn past the call's end, or `endedOnSilence` or `closed`, and no earlier recording queued or submitted.
