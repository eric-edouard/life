# derived/

Generated by Life; never hand-edit. Most of it can be deleted and rebuilt from the sources plus `~/Life` (`truth/` and `memory/`).
Logs are in `derived/log/YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl`; their `email.sent` lines are what keeps an alert from going out twice (`sendReport`), so deleting the last 8 days' logs re-sends alerts still due.

**State, not rebuildable: keep it.** `settings.json` (the engine, `bun life engine`), `memory/nightly.json`, `memory/backup.json` (the schedule's tries), `memory/midday.json` (the last midday slot used), `memory/backfill.json` (the backfill range and how far it read), `watch/cursor.json` (where the watcher is in lifelog's feed), `events.json` (the one MCP event subscription: its callback, secret and delivery cursor).
