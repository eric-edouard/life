# derived/

Generated by Life; never hand-edit. Most of it can be deleted and rebuilt from the sources plus `~/Life` (`truth/` and `memory/`).
Logs are in `derived/log/YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl`.

**State, not rebuildable: keep it.** `settings.json` (the engine, `bun life engine`), `memory/nightly.json`, `memory/backup.json` (the guards' tries), `memory/midday.json` (the last midday slot used), `board.json` (how Life left each Atlas board item: a lost one is a fresh baseline, so his changes since go unseen), `watch/cursor.json` (where the watcher is in lifelog's feed), `events.json` (the one MCP event subscription: its callback, secret and delivery cursor).
