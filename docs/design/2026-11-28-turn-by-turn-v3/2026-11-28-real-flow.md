# What really happens when the owner talks to Life

Reference: what the system does, moment by moment, when the owner asks Life something in the iPhone app or says "hey
Life" to the pendant, and what the phone can show at each moment. Written from the code and the live store on 28 Nov
2026, for the five [turn by turn v2](https://github.com/eric-edouard/life/blob/040db13018a0272148422c3a6de4d35aea305fa7/docs/design/2026-11-27-turn-by-turn-v2/index.html) prototypes, whose [scenario](https://github.com/eric-edouard/life/blob/040db13018a0272148422c3a6de4d35aea305fa7/docs/design/2026-11-27-turn-by-turn-v2/data.js) imagined a flow the system does
not have. Names below are made up.

## Measured timings

Read from the desk store (`desk.sqlite`, the `turn` and `thread_entry` tables), the decisions record, the ledger and
the day logs, 21 to 27 Nov 2026. Counts and durations only. The samples are small: 27 main-session turns, so treat
p90s as rough.

| What | Median | p90 | Sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| His typed or dictated ask, start of turn to answer done | 15 s | 36 s (max 51 s) | 25 answered turns |
| Start of turn to the session running | 2.8 s | 9.9 s | same |
| Start of turn to its first read of Life (the first step line) | 5.7 s | 18 s | 21 turns that read |
| One read of Life (`search`, `read_inbox`, `read_chats`…) | 0.1 s | 2.4 s | connector calls in those turns |
| Reads per turn | 2 | 7 | same |
| "Hey Life": end of his words to the ask reaching Life | 77 s | 32 min | 14 spoken asks since 1 Nov |
| An agent's turn that writes a draft card | 59 s | 3 min | 36 agent draft cards |
| The message that woke an agent to its draft card | 26 min | over a day | 27 of those cards |
| His Send to the message sent | 2.2 s | 6.3 s | 18 sends |

What the numbers say: searching Life is fast. Nearly all of a turn is the model thinking between reads. The slow
things are the pendant's words reaching the Mac and the background agents, not the answer.

## Who answers

Every ask goes to one place: Life's main session, one long-running model session (today Claude, Opus 5.5) that Life
keeps open across asks. Typed or dictated in Ask Life, the phone calls the connector's `ask_main_session`
([mcp-main-session.ts](../../../apps/server/src/host/http/mcp-main-session.ts#L76)), which hands his words and the refs
on his screen to the desk and relays what the session streams. A spoken "hey Life" enters the ledger as an ask and the
desk asks the same session ([spoken-ask-intake.ts](../../../apps/server/src/desk/spoken-ask-intake.ts#L112)). One ask at
a time: a second one waits its turn, marked with Life's 👀 at once
([main-session.ts:259](../../../apps/server/src/desk/main-session.ts#L259)). A turn has 5 minutes
([main-session.ts:89](../../../apps/server/src/desk/main-session.ts#L89)).

During the turn the session reads Life through the connector, in the words of the owner: searching Life. Its brief
([main-session.ts:86](../../../apps/server/src/desk/main-session.ts#L86)) points it at `fetch`, `read_inbox`,
`read_matters`, `read_people_and_organizations`, `read_chats`, `read_day`, `search`, `read_payments` and the rest. In the
measured turns the most used were `read_inbox`, `search`, `read_matters` and `read_people_and_organizations`. It can
also act on his words (his to-do list, a correction, a card, his agents), and hand long work to an agent with
`tell_agent`.

## What the phone receives while it works

Six kinds of line, and only these
([main-session-wire.ts:92](../../../apps/server/src/records/main-session-wire.ts#L92)):

| Line | What it carries | How the phone shows it |
|---|---|---|
| `thinking` | nothing | "Thinking…" before the first step |
| `step` | a verb and an object, written by code from the tool called | one line, replaced by the next |
| `words` | a chunk of the answer's Markdown | inked in as it comes |
| `block` | one of Life's components, once checked | drawn in place |
| `reaction` | 👀 or 👍 on his words | a mark on his bubble |
| `answer` | the whole answer, or an error | the entry settles |

A step is a fixed phrase per tool ([main-session-lines.ts:120](../../../apps/server/src/desk/main-session-lines.ts#L120)):
"Reading your day", "Reading your chats", "Looking up Sam", "Searching for “viewing”", "Preparing an ask", "Handing on
the work", and "Looking into it" for any other tool. It never says what the read found. The phone shows one step at a
time with a spinner, and the line goes when the words start
([ThreadView.swift:349](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Lifelog/Thread/ThreadView.swift#L349),
[ThreadComponents.swift:60](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Lifelog/DesignSystem/ThreadComponents.swift#L60)).

The answer is the session's text after its last tool call. Words written before a tool call are thrown away and start
again ([main-session.ts:503](../../../apps/server/src/desk/main-session.ts#L503)). So nothing of the answer can show
while Life is still reading: the words and blocks all come in the last seconds of the turn.

### The blocks an answer can hold

Eight kinds, fixed ([answer-blocks.ts:22](../../../apps/server/src/records/answer-blocks.ts#L22); the phone's copy,
[AnswerBlocks.swift:36](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Kit/Sources/LifeConnector/Thread/AnswerBlocks.swift#L36)).
Each is checked before it is drawn: its refs must open, and a value it quotes must be in its source as written. One that
fails is dropped ([main-session.ts:458](../../../apps/server/src/desk/main-session.ts#L458)).

| Block | Carries |
|---|---|
| `items` | 1 to 8 refs of things that wait: an ask or card from his inbox, a matter, a step. Drawn live, with their own buttons (a draft card shows its draft and Send). |
| `pictures` | 1 to 20 photo refs Life holds |
| `table` | columns, up to 40 rows, optional total, each value checked against `from` |
| `document` | a receipt, booking, ticket or invoice: title, up to 8 lines as written, optional total |
| `phrase` | words for him to say to someone, in their language, with his own |
| `place` | a name and an address; a tap opens Maps |
| `question` | 2 to 4 likely answers he can tap, one recommended |
| `settings` | Life's settings, or one setting offered as a switch |

There is no block for a calendar event, a person's face, a quoted chat message, a payment, the weather, a route or a
countdown. Those can only be words, a `table`, a `place`, or an `items` row pointing at a card or matter.

## The scenarios

### A question about his day, typed

1. He sends "What's on today?". The phone shows his bubble and "Thinking…" at once, from its own state
   ([LifeThread.swift:182](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Kit/Sources/LifeConnector/Thread/LifeThread.swift#L182)).
   0 s.
2. The session starts (median 2.8 s) and reads: "Reading your day", maybe "Reading what waits on you". First step
   around 6 s.
3. It writes the answer: a short line, then perhaps a `table` of the day's events from `read_day`, an `items` block
   for what waits on him. Done around 15 s, 36 s on a bad turn.

### "Did Jade answer?", held to talk in the app

1. He holds the button. If his dictation switch is on instant, his words show in the field as he speaks (ElevenLabs'
   realtime transcription, [LiveWords.swift:43](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Kit/Sources/ServerAPI/LiveWords.swift#L43));
   otherwise the Mac hears the recording after. Then the same as typed.
2. Steps: "Looking up Jade", "Reading your chats". Each read takes a fraction of a second.
3. Answer: words ("Yes, at 9:12: she can book for 8"). No quote block exists, so her message is quoted in the words.
   If an agent already drafted a reply to her (next scenario but one), the session can show that card in an `items`
   block, with its Send. It does not write a draft just because he asked a question. About 15 s.

### "Tell Jade I'll be late"

1. As above, then "Looking up Jade", "Reading your chats", "Preparing an ask".
2. The session asks a card as his own (`request_decision`, asker `owner`): the exact message, in his usual way of
   writing to her, with up to 3 quick edits like "Warmer" ([card-request.ts:84](../../../apps/server/src/memory/card-request.ts#L84)).
   Cards that follow his own ask are never held by the gate ([gate.ts:261](../../../apps/server/src/desk/gate.ts#L261)).
   In the two measured cases the card was asked 32 s and 45 s into the turn.
3. The answer shows the card in an `items` block. Life never sends on his words alone.
4. He taps the send option. Life checks nobody wrote in the chat since, sends through the chat's own bridge, and waits
   to see it sent: median 2.2 s ([draft-send.ts:441](../../../apps/server/src/memory/draft-send.ts#L441)). The card shows
   sent. Where the chat allows deleting for everyone, an Undo deletes it and says what Jade will see
   ([answer-result.ts:677](../../../apps/server/src/host/answer-result.ts#L677)); iMessage and mail have no Undo.

### A pre-drafted card arriving while he is in the app

Drafts the owner did not ask for come from agents, in the background, never from the main session.

1. Jade writes. The watcher's arrival reader reads new chat lines every 5 minutes
   ([watch.ts:322](../../../apps/server/src/host/watch.ts#L322)); an agent waiting on that chat hears it at the desk's
   next poll, every 30 s ([desk-helper.ts:42](../../../apps/server/src/desk/desk-helper.ts#L42)).
2. One agent wakes, reads the chat and its matter, and asks a card with a draft. Its turn: median 1 minute, 3 at p90.
   From her message to the card: median 26 minutes in the sample.
3. The gate reads the card against what he is doing now and may hold it for a better moment: up to 3 hours, 12 for a
   low one ([gate.ts:33](../../../apps/server/src/desk/gate.ts#L33)).
4. Delivered, it pushes once. In the app, the push makes the phone read its thread again, and the new card appears
   unfolded at the bottom of the thread, led by its context
   ([RootView.swift:135](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Lifelog/RootView.swift#L135),
   [OpeningScreen.swift:25](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Kit/Sources/LifeConnector/Thread/OpeningScreen.swift#L24)).
   Sending is as above. If he answers Jade himself in WhatsApp first, Life withdraws the card within 30 s.

Volume: 58 agent draft cards from 18 to 27 Nov, against 20 drafts he asked for.

### A slow ask

There is no slow step to show. The reads are fast; what is slow is the model reasoning or a long chain of reads, and
the phone sees only the current step line. Past 5 minutes the turn is cancelled and he reads "I couldn't finish that."
([main-session.ts:90](../../../apps/server/src/desk/main-session.ts#L90)). Work that cannot fit a reply (a booking, a
search across the web, research) is handed to an agent: the answer says so and the thread shows a row under way with
how long that kind of work takes now ([ThreadView.swift:373](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Lifelog/Thread/ThreadView.swift#L373),
[main-session.ts:239](../../../apps/server/src/desk/main-session.ts#L239)). The result comes later as a late reply in the
thread, or as a card.

### A failure

What can fail, and what he sees:

- No Mac or no internet: his ask stays on the phone as waiting, and goes by itself when it can
  ([LifeThread.swift:12](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Kit/Sources/LifeConnector/Thread/LifeThread.swift#L12)).
- The desk off or unreachable: "Life's agents did not answer. Try again in a moment."
  ([LifeThread.swift:43](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Kit/Sources/LifeConnector/Thread/LifeThread.swift#L44)).
- Over 5 minutes: "I couldn't finish that." A tap on his bubble asks again.
- A read that finds nothing is not a failure: the session answers in words that it found nothing.

The main session has no timer of its own: it cannot promise "I'll check again at 18:00". It can hand that to an agent,
or record an instruction. Payments are read from what Life already synced from his money app, never from his bank
live ([payments/backend.ts](../../../apps/server/src/payments/backend.ts)), so "couldn't reach your bank" is not a
failure the owner can see.

### "Hey Life" from the pendant

1. He speaks. Nothing shows on the phone: there is no live transcription of the pendant.
2. The pendant's audio reaches the phone, then the Mac, which transcribes it. Life hears "hey Life" in the words
   ([hear.ts:127](../../../apps/server/src/host/hear.ts#L127)); a wake with no speaker yet waits up to 40 s for his name.
   From the end of his words to the ask in Life: median 77 s, p90 about half an hour (the phone or pendant out of
   reach).
3. Within 5 s the desk opens his thread entry with his words and 👀, and pushes "Life heard you · On it."
   ([spoken-ask-intake.ts:150](../../../apps/server/src/desk/spoken-ask-intake.ts#L154)).
4. The main session answers as for a typed ask (about 15 s), but nothing streams to the phone. The push is replaced
   by "Life answered" or "Life couldn't do it", with "Open Life to read it." and none of the words. Words heard with
   low confidence, or said over an hour before, run nothing and end on a line saying so.

From the end of his sentence to the answer: about 1.5 to 2 minutes typically, much longer when the phone was out of
reach. As of 28 Nov no pendant ask has gone end to end through the main session in the live store: all 10 thread
entries are asks from the app.

## What is instant, and what needs a turn

Instant, from the phone's own copy (Life's thread read, kept on disk for a cold start,
[LifeStore.swift:175](../../../apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Lifelog/Life/LifeStore.swift#L175)): the thread with its
answers, the open asks and cards with their drafts and buttons, what is broken, the opening screen's top 3 and the
count. The copy is refreshed on opening the app, on each card push and after each answer, not continuously.

A second or two, no model: anything opened by a tap. A person's page, a matter, a `life:` link, an appointment are
direct connector reads.

A main-session turn, 15 s typical: anything asked in words, the day included. The phone holds no day view of its own.

## What the five prototypes must change

1. Steps carry no findings. Drop "3 events today", "Electricity, €84", stations with "live slivers" (E), blocks that
   fill as each step ends (B), objects that turn over as their step ends (D). The real thing is one line, "Reading your
   chats", replaced at each read, gone when the answer starts.
2. Steps follow tools, not topics. A turn may read the inbox first and the chats second; there is no fixed plan of
   steps known in advance, so no skeleton or face-down layout can stand before the first step.
3. The answer comes at the end, in a burst. Design for 5 to 30 s of one changing step line, then words and blocks
   arriving within a second or two.
4. Use the eight blocks, or propose a new one marked "new block" (decided 28 Nov): an event, a face, a quoted message,
   a payment. Still no route map or countdown ring: Life has no route lookup. A place opens Maps.
5. Drafts are cards, not part of an answer. "Drafting an answer in your words" during a question does not happen. A
   draft appears when he asks to send something (the session's "Preparing an ask", then an `items` block with the
   card) or arrives on its own from an agent, minutes after the message, as a card brought into the thread.
6. Life does not speak. Nothing in the app reads answers aloud. Variant C's "spoken lines" can only be text paced on
   screen, and the pacing would be invented: the words arrive as fast as the model writes them.
7. Voice in the app: live words in the field are real (with the instant setting). Voice on the pendant: no live words,
   no listening state on the phone, the ask lands a minute or more later, and its answer is reached from a push.
8. No live outside lookups that Life lacks: no bank call, no route time, no "leave by 9:55". The session could search
   the web for weather, slowly; that is untested and not something to design around.
9. Failures are of the turn, not of a step: waiting for the Mac, "did not answer", "couldn't finish". No promise of a
   retry at a time.
10. A slow ask is a long turn or work handed to an agent, shown as a row under way with an estimate, answered later in
    the thread. There is no "your mail goes back years" step.
11. A second ask while one runs is accepted and marked 👀; he can stop the running one. An act done may answer with 👍
    alone.

## Decided (28 Nov 2026)

The owner's answers to this page's questions:

1. Keep the step line: one quiet line that changes with each read while Life searches
   ([What Life shows](../../product.md#L64), rule 8).
2. When he asks about a message an agent already drafted a reply to, the answer always brings that draft card, Send
   one tap away.
3. Prototypes may propose new answer blocks (an event, a face, a quoted message, a payment…), each marked "new block"
   so we know what to build. Timing and flow stay real.
4. Life never speaks aloud: text only.
