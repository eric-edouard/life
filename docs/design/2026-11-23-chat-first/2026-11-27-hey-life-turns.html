Hey Life, turn by turn

A proposal, not approved. You say “hey Life” and your ask to the pendant or the phone. Your phone tells you it was heard. A tap opens the app on that ask, as your turn, and Life answers it there. Made-up people.

How it moves

  1. Heard: the notification comes in as soon as Life hears the ask: within seconds once “hey Life” is heard live, later today (see Honest timing). It quotes your ask, so you know it was heard right.
  2. One notification, never two: when the answer is ready, the same notification is replaced by the answer’s first line. Not tapped, it is still one item on your lock screen.
  3. The tap: the app opens straight on your turn: your words, centred, with where you said them (“To your pendant · 14:02”). Nothing from before shows; the last turns are one scroll up.
  4. Life working: the light gathers in the centre under your words, and one line says what Life is reading (“Reading your chat with Jade”). The line changes in place, a cross-fade, never a list.
  5. The answer: your words shrink to the top, the light settles, and Life’s answer rises into the middle, its card last. The next ask’s suggestion fills the field.
  6. Slow: after about 20 seconds the line adds “You can close the app. I’ll tell you when it’s ready.” Closing it changes nothing; the notification carries the answer.
  7. Not heard right: what was caught shows as your turn, the unclear part faded, with Say it again first and Type it beside it. Your words are never guessed.
  8. Offline: your ask is kept, never lost and never to be repeated. It shows as your turn with “Waiting for your Mac”, the edge goes still, and Life answers on its own when the Mac is back.
  9. Every ask ends somewhere you can see: answered, done, or couldn’t, with why and what next. Silence is never an outcome.
  10. Honest timing: “heard” comes as early as Life can hear it. Today that is when your words are written down, which can take half an hour, so the notification and your turn say when you said it. Until then, the app says it is still writing down, never that it is working on your ask.
  11. Under Reduce Motion every step is a cross-fade.

Decisions for you

  1. A notification, or a Live Activity? Drawn as one notification replaced by the answer. A Live Activity would show Life working on the lock screen too, with the line that changes; it costs more to build and stays on screen until the answer.
  2. Answer read aloud? When you asked by voice with the phone locked, Life could read the answer’s first line through your earphones. Drawn without it.
  3. Hear “hey Life” live? Today Life finds it in the written words, minutes after you spoke. Hearing it live (the pendant or the phone listening for it) would make “heard” come within seconds; it is a build of its own.
  4. From the phone too? Drawn for the pendant, and the phone’s own “hey Life” the same way, with “To your phone” under your words.
