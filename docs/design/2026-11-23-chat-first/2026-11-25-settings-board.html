3CSetting up "Life only": a checklist, app by app. Each line opens iOS Settings at that app (where iOS allows; otherwise it shows the path). An app Life can't read stays on, said plainly. What I'd keep

1C + 1B: choices live on the person or matter, and you change them by talking. 2B + 2C: the pill warns you inside Life, the phone warns you when Life is down; 2A only as the place the fix lives. 3A then 3C: one card at setup, smart by default, and the checklist only if you pick "Life only". 3B as the same per-person choices of 1C, seen all at once, reached from Settings.
