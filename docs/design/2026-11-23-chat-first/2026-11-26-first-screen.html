  6. "3 more" turns into the 3 rows, which slide in below; nothing above moves.
  7. Show earlier brings the last conversation in from above, a day at a time, as in the prototype. The greeting and the rows stay where they are.
  8. Asking something from the clean screen: your words land under the rows and the thread scrolls as always. The greeting and rows stay above; at the next clean screen they become history.

Decisions for you

  1. Your name in the greeting? You asked for your first name; the brand's wording rule says the screen never uses your own name. Drawn with the name, and the rule would gain this one exception.
  2. Is 3 the right cap? 3 fit on screen with two broken things and the field, and stay readable at a glance. Only what blocks you counts (due within 3 days, or marked important); the rest stays behind the count, as now.
  3. Is 5 minutes the right wait? It covers a quick trip to another app (a code, a call, a reply) without losing your place; 10 would keep more context, 2 would clean more often.
  4. A broken thing: a row you tap, or open already? Drawn as a row that opens in place with its one button, like every ask. The other way: when it is the only thing on screen, it arrives already open.
