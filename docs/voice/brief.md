# Brief

What the product is for. How it is built is in `architecture.md`; where a decision in `decisions.md` differs, the
decision is current.

## Purpose

Life reliably keeps the owner's recorded life and turns that audio into meaningful conversations: easy to find,
understand, replay and export. The owner speaks English, French and Thai, switching within
conversations and sentences. The audio is wearable, real-world audio (distant voices, street and restaurant noise,
overlap), not dictation.

It answers:

- Is my Pendant actually recording, and how trustworthy is that answer?
- Is my audio safe, and what is still waiting to be transferred?
- What conversations happened, who was involved, where, and what mattered?
- Can I get back to an exact moment through audio or text?
- Can I keep and move my data without depending on this app or a provider?

Priorities, in order:

1. Recoverable capture and truthful status.
2. Transcription accuracy and useful conversation understanding.
3. Easy retrieval and listening.
4. Polished everyday use.
5. Measured resource efficiency and controlled cost.

Speed of processing never wins over accuracy.

## Speakers and people

- Diarization separates speakers within a recording; recognition links them to persistent people.
- A match needs enough clean voice evidence; similar embeddings alone are not proof, and ambiguity stays unknown.
- Only the owner creates people and adds voice evidence. Manual corrections survive all later processing.
- Recurring unnamed voices are never presented as confirmed people.
- Recognition never triggers another paid transcription. Removing a profile never deletes recordings.

## Character

Calm, expressive and concise. No generic AI copy, redundant status or needless warnings; permissions, privacy, cost and
actionable errors get plain explanations. Health reads without relying on color.
