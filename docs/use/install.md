# Install Life

How-to: put Life on your Mac and your iPhone. It takes about twenty minutes, most of it waiting for downloads. You need
a Mac with macOS 14 or later, an iPhone, and the invitation the person who gave you Life sent you.

## On your Mac

1. Download `Life-<version>.dmg` from the link you were given and open it.
2. Drag **Life** onto **Applications**, then open it from there. The first time, macOS says it was downloaded from the
   internet; press **Open**. Life is signed and checked by Apple, so nothing else is asked.
3. Life appears in the menu bar as an **L**. Its setup window opens. Answer it step by step: it asks only for what
   your computer and your choices need.

You do not need Terminal, git, Homebrew or any developer tool. Life carries its own git, Google command line and
messaging bridges inside the app, and keeps itself up to date: when a new version is out, Life offers it from its menu.

Life does not copy your data to anyone. What leaves your Mac, and to whom, is listed in
[SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md#what-leaves-the-mac).

## On your iPhone

1. Open the email from TestFlight, Apple's app for trying apps before they are released, and install TestFlight if you
   do not have it. The invitation arrives once the person who gave you Life has added you.
2. In TestFlight, press **Install** next to Life.
3. Open Life. When Life's setup window on your Mac shows a QR code, scan it with the phone. The two now know each other.

## When something does not work

- macOS says Life cannot be opened: you opened the copy inside the disk image. Drag it to **Applications** and open that one.
- The QR code does not scan: check that the Mac and the phone are online, then ask the setup window for a new code.
- Anything else: write to the person who gave you Life, with what you saw on the screen.
