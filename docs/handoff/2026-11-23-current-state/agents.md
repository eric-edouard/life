## 1. The desk

What it is: a helper process (`life desk`, `desk/desk-helper.ts` function `desk`) that owns every registered agent. It is off until the owner's `desk` setting (`off("desk")`, `core/outputs.ts`). Its state is one SQLite file, `desk.sqlite`, never `memory/`. Layer rule: nothing imports the desk; `desk/life-entry.ts` is the command line entry.

Start-up (`desk/desk-helper.ts`): takes a lock (`hold("desk")`), runs the store steps (`stepStore`, `stepRoles`, `stepTurnModel`, `stepMatterIds`, `stepMoves`, `stepRoleCheck`, `stepTurnSearchCost`, all in `desk/store-step.ts`; each makes a dated copy first and is meant to be deleted once run), opens the store, opens the door (`openDoor`), then loops: `tick(...)` then sleep `TICK_MS` = 5 s. Every `POLL_EVERY` = 6th tick is a poll tick. A tick longer than 5 minutes (`HUNG_AFTER`) logs `run.error` step `crash` and exits so the app supervisor restarts it.

Store shape: tables in `desk/desk-store.ts` `SCHEMA` (about line 82 on): `agent`, `assignment`, `session`, `event`, `turn`, `cursor`, `anchor`, `report_line`, `agent_change`, `card`, `handed_step`, plus `move`, `runtime_block` (lines 28 to 55). Read only through host: `host/desk-read.ts` (`readDesk`, `agentOf`, `openAssignmentsOf`, `runtimeOf`, `modelOf`, `MAX_ACTIVE` = 50 at line 114), `host/agents-now.ts` (`agentsNow`, `amberOf`), `host/registered-agents.ts` (`readRegisteredAgents`), `host/desk-turn-reads.ts`, `host/move-read.ts`.

The tick (`desk/desk-clock.ts` `tick`, line ~676), in order:
1. `applyAgentChanges` (`desk/agent-changes.ts`): the owner's word about agents (start, stop, resume, re-point, rename, change model, `hand_over`, `continue_on_mac`), queued by the connector or main session as `agent_change` rows.
2. `advanceMoves` (`desk/agent-moves.ts`): hand-overs of a matter to another agent, a step at a time, with rollback.
3. `standUp("main", MAIN_BRIEF)` and `standUp("caretaker", CARETAKER_BRIEF)` (`desk/role-agents.ts`): register a role agent if none is active or stopped.
4. `receiveComplaints` (`desk/complaint-intake.ts`): the only writer of the complaint record; opens the caretaker's assignment.
5. `receiveSpokenAsks` (`desk/spoken-ask-intake.ts`): "hey Life" asks go to one agent (closeness in meaning to a matter, else Personal).
6. `handOutNext` (`desk/hand-out-tick.ts`).
7. `pollAnswers` (`desk/desk-clock.ts` line 271): his answers to cards become events for the asking agent.
8. `endUnbegunTurns`, then `wakeDue`: starts due turns, at most `STARTS_PER_TICK` = 3 (`desk/agent-wait.ts`), ordered by `dueOf` rank then time.
9. On poll ticks only: `writeRuntimeBlocks`, `pollMessages` (then `pollArrivals`), `pollCalls`, `pollChannels` (`desk/event-queue.ts`), `pollRecordings` (`desk/matter-net.ts`), `endAgentsOnOverMatters`, `foldAssignments`, `endAgentsWithoutCard`, `endLostTurns`.

Agents and roles. `AgentRole` = `personal | caretaker | main` (`core/names.ts:89`); a project agent has no role. Status `active | stopped | handed_over | ended` (`records/registered-agent.ts:107`). One active or stopped agent per role at most (`desk/role-agents.ts` `standingAgentOf`, `standUp`, `roleAssignmentOf`). Main and caretaker are registered at desk start with no assignment; they run no turn until his words or a complaint open one; they take two of the 50 active places. The Personal agent is registered by the desk only when a confirmed arrival or spoken ask has no matter agent. `hasFixedSubject` (caretaker, main) and `isWokenOnlyByHisWords` (main) are in `records/registered-agent.ts:110-115`. Briefs: `PERSONAL_BRIEF` (`desk/role-agents.ts:14`), `MAIN_BRIEF` (`desk/main-session.ts:62`), `CARETAKER_BRIEF` (`desk/complaint-intake.ts:45`). Agents are known at the door by an HMAC key derived from the id (`desk/door-keys.ts`), never by name; the app shows no role names.

Assignments: a matter is never an agent, it is an assignment of the agent that takes it (`agentForOf`, `memory/held-matters.ts`; `agentForMatterOf`, `memory/read-matters.ts`). `opened_by` is `owner` or `desk` (`OpenedBy`, `records/registered-agent.ts:118`). The assignment's first turn must ask a plan card before `set_wait` or `finish` (door rule, `turnRefusalOf` in `desk/desk-door.ts:~110`; exceptions: caretaker, and Personal holding his spoken ask, `isHisWordsPlanOf`, `host/inbox.ts:33`). An assignment whose first turn never began within `BEGIN_WITHIN_MS` (5 min, `desk/agent-wait.ts:83`) is closed, not retried. A hand-out assignment with no card in `CARD_WITHIN_MS` (2 h, `desk/hand-out.ts:45`) is closed (`endAgentsWithoutCard`).

Waits and wakes. An agent's wait is `AgentWait` = `events` (up to 20 `EventFilter`s), `until` (5 min to 7 days ahead), `reason` (`records/registered-agent.ts:25-103`), set with the door's `set_wait`. Pure rules in `desk/agent-wait.ts` (`waitOf`, `defaultWaitOf`, `nextWakeOf`, `EVENTS_SETTLE` 5 min, retries `MAX_HANDED`). Wake causes: the deadline, a matching event, his answer to a card, a card's withdrawal, `call.ended`, `ask.spoken`, `arrival.confirmed`, `matter.related` (wide net, `desk/matter-net.ts:54`), `draft.contested`, `draft.kept`, `complaint.received`. `WakeWhy` (`desk/event-queue.ts:57`) is `wait | person | meaning`. Event channels a wait may name: `payments`, `places`, `github_activity` (`host/event-channels.ts` lines 159, 233, 294), plus messages by source `incoming`. One matcher: `matcherOf` / `EventFilter` (`host/events.ts`). A wake hands facts, never message text. Routing: `routeOf`, `matterOrPersonalOf`, `agentWakesOf` in `desk/event-queue.ts`.

Hand-out: `handOutOf` (`desk/hand-out.ts:37`, pure) over `handOutGateOf` (`memory/read-matters.ts:427`), run by `handOutNext` (`desk/hand-out-tick.ts`), paced by `paceRefusalOf` with the settings `desk.handOut` = `{aDay, cardsWaiting}` (`core/settings.ts:84`). Opens an assignment on the agent that takes the matter; it starts no agent by itself.

The fold: `foldAssignments` (`desk/matter-net.ts:464`) moves each open matter assignment to the agent that takes it (with wait, notes, cards) when its agent has no turn running; an agent left with nothing ends. Matters moved by `hand_over` are pinned (`pinnedAssignmentsOf`) and not folded. `endOutcomeOf` (`desk/matter-net.ts:125`) is the one rule for closing an assignment when its matter is over.

Runtimes: `RuntimeName` = `claude | codex | pi` (`core/names.ts`). Contract `desk/runtimes/contract.ts`; one implementation `sessionRuntime` (`desk/runtimes/session-runtime.ts`) over a connection per protocol: ACP for claude and codex (`desk/runtimes/acp-runtime.ts`, rows in `desk/runtimes/runtime-rows.ts`, `runtimeRowsOf` line 470) and pi's RPC (`desk/runtimes/pi-runtime.ts`, `piRuntime()`; extension `pi-extension.ts` runs the send guard inside pi; `pi-subagent.ts`, `web-tools.ts`). Registry: `RUNTIMES` in `desk/desk-helper.ts:30`. Which runtime an agent gets: `runtimeOf` (`host/desk-read.ts:545`): the start's named runtime, else `settings().desk.roleRuntimes[role]`, else `desk.defaultRuntime`, else `BUILT_IN_RUNTIME` = pi (`core/names.ts:96`). When the home runtime can not run, an agent with no session yet takes pi (`desk/runtime-fallback.ts`, `runnableAgentOf`); a session is never moved. An agent resumes its one session on every wake. The desk does not resume a Claude session a live process holds (`isSessionOpen`, `runtime-rows.ts`); a holder quiet 10 minutes is taken back (`takeBackIfLeft`, `desk-clock.ts`).

Model per agent: `modelOf` (`host/desk-read.ts:552`): the agent's own `model`/`effort`, else `settings().desk.runtimes[runtime]`, else the runtime's own default. For pi, the model is not a desk setting: `lifeProviderOf` (`desk/runtimes/pi-runtime.ts:153`) and `piRowOf` (line 340) use `piRouteOf()` (`models/llm.ts:155`), the memory's OpenRouter route for the main tier: `OPENROUTER_MODELS.main = routed("high")` (`models/llm.ts:143-147`), i.e. `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017` on OpenRouter hosts `deepinfra/fp8` and `morph/fp8`, effort high, context `PROVIDERS.openrouter.contextTokens` (1,048,576), Life's own OpenRouter key (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, `pi-runtime.ts:139`). So pi agents and the memory pipeline share one model, set in `models/llm.ts`. Claude and codex agents run on his plans with his own settings. Defaults for the other tiers: `models/llm.ts:43-65`.

Desk-level other: web search for pi agents, `web-search/backend.ts`, order `desk.webSearch` else `WEB_SEARCH_CHAIN`. Apify reads `desk/apify-reads.ts`. `continue_on_mac`: `desk/continue-on-mac.ts`. Plugin lookup `/session`: `desk/desk-door.ts` (`SESSION_PATH`). Complaint receipts: `desk/complaint-intake.ts`.

## 2. The main session (Ask Life)

Path: phone app (connector, his token only) calls `ask_main_session` (`host/http/mcp-main-session.ts:77`) with `words` and `screen` (refs only: `tab`, `open`, up to 20 `visible`; `MainSessionScreen`, `records/main-session-wire.ts:8`). The tool is refused to paired agents and when the desk is off. `askDesk` (line ~30) POSTs `MainSessionAsk` to the desk door's loopback `/main-session` (`MAIN_SESSION_PATH`, `host/http/http.ts:55`; handler near `desk/desk-door.ts:577`) with the local key, and relays an NDJSON stream as progress notifications; the call ends on the answer. With no `words` it only tells the desk he is on the screen (session kept warm). The tap's `life/answerKey` makes a retry ask once (`askMainSession`, `desk/main-session.ts:376`; `ASKS_KEPT` 20, a repeat key with other words errors).

The session: role `main`, one at most (`desk/main-session.ts`, `keepMainSession` line 239). Each ask is one desk turn, cause `MAIN_ASK` = `main_ask`, outcome `replied`, on one session the runtime keeps across asks (`Runtime.talk`). Runtime from `runtimeOf({role: "main"})`, so `roleRuntimes.main` or default. It is "one of his own sessions in full" (his settings, skills, connectors), with the send guard on. Its brief is `MAIN_BRIEF` (`desk/main-session.ts:62`): two short sentences, his language, the key word in `**`.

Door tools (`desk/desk-door.ts`): `MAIN_TOOLS` (line 148) = `answer`, `change_appointment`, `change_directory_entry`, `change_registered_agent`, `change_todo_item`, `complain`, `correct_memory`, `show`, `tell_agent`, `tell_life`; plus `request_decision` (card asked as `owner`, no agent woken by the answer). On a non-main turn these are refused (`turnRefusalOf`), except the caretaker's `correct_memory` (`isCaretakerFix`). Each act takes `said`, found in his words of that ask by `hisWordsIn` (`desk/main-session.ts:106`): it proves the words are his, not that the act matches. Tool registrations: `answer` line ~483, `show` ~504, `tell_agent` ~350, `correct_memory` ~367, `complain` ~464.

Wire (`records/main-session-wire.ts`):
- `AnswerExtras` (`answer` tool): `why` (300 chars), `sources` (up to 5 refs), `answers` (2 to 4 likely answers, makes it a question).
- `MainSessionShow` (line 46), what `show` can express, changes no data: `{kind:"page", page: agent|matter|person|project, id}` or `{kind:"list", list: checks|matters|people, label (60 chars), refs (1 to 50)}`. Checked when shown by `showProblemOf` (`desk/main-session.ts:161`): a list's refs must be rows that tab shows now (`LIST_ROWS`: `inboxChecksNow`, `readMatterRowsNow`, `readPeopleAndOrganizations`); a page must open (a matter by `refsProblemOf`, a person, a project in `truth.projects`, an agent that is not main).
- `MainSessionAct` (recorded by the door from what each act wrote): `done` (optional `matter`), `agent` (name and `told`), `card` (id), `fix` (complaint received by caretaker).
- `MainSessionAnswer`: `reply` (`words`, `why`, `sources`), `question` (`question`, `answers`), an act kind with `words`, or `error` (`what`). `answerOf` (`desk/main-session.ts:194`) picks: question wins over act over reply. Any non-error kind may carry `show`.
- `MainSessionLine` streamed first: `thinking`, `working` (`id`, `title`, `state`), `words` (`text`), then `answer`.

What the phone receives: the stream lines then one answer; `offline` is the phone's own state. His complaint ("Wrong..." on a card, or his words) reaches the caretaker only through the main session's `complain` (`complainThroughAskLife`, `host/complaints.ts`), with receipt `reached: "caretaker"`.

## 3. Cards (decisions and checks)

A card is a decision (`memory/decisions.ts`, append-only `decisions.jsonl`) or one of Life's own checks (`memory/checks.ts`, `checksNow`; kinds in `CheckGroup`, `core/names.ts`: decision, voice, name, contacts card, same person, guessed person, at organization, new matter, your move, ending). Decisions are the only agent-asked kind; he sees all open ones in the inbox.

Asking: an agent calls the door's `request_decision` (`desk/desk-door.ts:283`, implemented by `askDecision`, `desk/agent-actions.ts:490`) or the connector's `request_decision` (`host/http/mcp.ts:568`, his token or a paired agent's); both end in `requestDecision` (`memory/decisions.ts:377`). Door asks are two-step: first call returns a preview as his phone shows it (`memory/card-preview.ts`), repeat unchanged with `previewed` (a digest) to ask. Shape: `Ask` (`memory/card-request.ts:57`): `title` 80, `question` 120, `options` 2 to 4, `context` (required Markdown with `life:<ref>` links, each must open), `links`, `deadline` (1 hour to 14 days), `onSilence`, `subject` (matter, step, `project:`, `area:`), optional `draft`, `needs`, `doneSeenBy` (`payment` or `tap`), `plan` (up to 12 `PlanStep`), `gathered`, `merges`. Rules text `CARD` / `CARD_SELF_CHECK` (line 108-111); detail pages by `fetch("ask/<name>")` (`askPageOf`). Refusals in `memory/card-problems.ts` (plan card needs `plan` steps, a first card on a finished matter is no plan). Plan card options `PLAN_OPTIONS` (`start`, `decline`).

Needs (`memory/needs.ts`, one registry `KINDS`): `account`, `amount`, `apify`, `app`, `event`, `link`, `place`, `qr`, `text`. `line` is worked out when read (`needLineOf`). `APIFY_RUN_MAX_USD` = 20.

Option kinds (`Option`, `memory/card-request.ts:19`): `outgoing` (an act that leaves Life, never taken on silence; on a draft card it is the send), `adds` (writes the `event` need into his calendar once: `addEventByHisWord`, `host/card-event-calendar.ts`), `runs` (starts the `apify` need's run once: `runApifyByHisWord`, `host/card-apify-run.ts`), `ends` (`step` or `matter`: his word it is done, `endByHisWord`), `looks` (Life looks for the step done, `lookForDone`, `host/done-seen.ts`, payments only), `takes: "note"`, `icon`. Withdrawal and keep shapes: `Withdrawal`, `Keep` (`card-request.ts:93-101`).

Answering: the connector's `answer_check` (`host/http/mcp.ts:584`; his app token only, `NOT_HIS` for agents) goes through `answerWithResult` (`host/answer-result.ts:560`) to `memory/answer-check.ts`, which per kind calls its own writer; a decision goes to `answerDecision` (`memory/decisions.ts:649`) with `add`, `end`, `look`, `run`, `deliver` hooks and `answerProblemOf` (line 540) validating the pick. A repeat of an answered card's same answer returns the first result and sends nothing; another answer is refused. He may answer in his own words (`note`). The asking agent is woken by `pollAnswers`.

Drafts and sending (`memory/draft.ts`, `memory/draft-send.ts`): a draft is text, one file or a reaction, with the replied-to message and mentions (`MessageDraft`, `StoredDraft`, `draftProblemOf`, `isSendable`). `resolveDraft` (`draft-send.ts:290`) resolves exactly one chat when the card is asked and stamps what he sees. `sendDraft` (line 441) sends only the stored line, once, on his pick of the card's outgoing option, through the source's send path (`Descriptor.send`, `sources/contract.ts`): Matrix bridges `matrixSend` (`sources/matrix.ts`), iMessage `messagesAppSend` (`sources/messages-app.ts`), mail through gog (`sources/gmail.ts`), GitHub through `gh` (`sources/github.ts`). Outcomes `not sent:` (card reopens, only his new answer sends again) and `may have gone:` (stays answered). `wroteSince` (line 364) refuses a send when anyone wrote in the chat since the card or his last attempt; `withdrawDraftsAnsweredInChat` (`decisions.ts:301`, watcher every 30 s) withdraws a card he answered in the chat himself. Send steps for progress: `sendStepsOf` (line 169). One open draft per chat; contests handled by `memory/draft-contest.ts` (`CONTEST_WAIT` 10 min; holder yields, merges or keeps).

Send guard (`desk/send-guard.ts`): a `PreToolUse` hook (`SEND_GUARD_HOOK`, matcher `Bash|mcp__.*`) run before each shell and MCP call of a registered agent of his own Life: `sendRefusalOf` (line 199) refuses messaging sites and apps, send/reply/forward tool names, `gog gmail send`, `imsg send`, osascript Messages/Mail send, computer use, `gh issue/pr comment` outside allowed repos, calendar attendees, and his connector's writes (`CONNECTOR_WRITES`, `core/names.ts:31`) on any server but the agent's own `desk` door. Facts per row: `SendFacts` (own addresses, post repos) passed in env `LIFE_SEND_FACTS` (`runtime-rows.ts:161`). In pi it runs inside pi's process (`pi-extension.ts`); in claude/codex as a command hook (`desk/send-guard-hook.ts`, Codex trusted by hash). It is text matching and partial by design (`SECURITY.md` lists what gets past). A second Life's agent (`isOwnLife` false) gets only its door.

## 4. What reaches the owner, and in what order

Checks read: `openChecksNow` / `inboxChecksNow` (`host/inbox.ts:109-116`) = `checksNow` (`memory/checks.ts`) over steps an agent is anchored on; cache key `checksKeyOf` (stats of the files it reads, his day, open decisions, anchored steps, the queued voices). Connector read: `read_inbox` (`host/http/mcp.ts:531`, `readInbox` in `host/inbox.ts:150`).

Order (`memory/check-urgency.ts`): kind rank `RANK` (line ~17): decision 0; contacts card, name, voice 1; your move 2; same person 3; guessed person, at organization 4; new matter 5; ending 6 (`inboxRankOf`, a voice with a lifelog suggestion ranks as guessed person). `inboxOrderOf` (line 129): blocking tier before improving; caretaker-fixed (`beingFixed`) cards last; important matter first; then card with a deadline, soonest first; then kind rank; then within kind (`withinKind`: decisions and your move oldest first; others by matters, lines, pages, newest); a matter's cards are grouped after its first (`matterKeyOf`). Ending checks do not show in the inbox, only on the matter page (`isInboxCheck`). Your move cards are ordered among themselves by their matter's rank.

Blocking and badge: `urgencyOf` (line 53) is `blocks` for first-tier kinds (rank up to your move) unless the caretaker is on it; a your move card blocks only when its deadline is within 3 days (`DUE_SOON_DAYS`). `blockingCountOf` = the app badge; `inboxOf` also gives counts per group and `deadlinesToday`.

Push (`host/card-push.ts`): the watcher's minute runs `pushNewCardsIfChanged` (line 226) which calls `pushNewCards` (line 193): every new open card pushes once (a subject is notified once, `cardSubjectOf`; id is `pushId`), in inbox order; up to `ALONE` = 5 of Life's own new checks push alone, the rest share one push, an agent's decision always alone; first run pushes nothing; cards no longer open are removed from the phone (`REMOVED_MAX` 100); `seen-cards.json` in the watcher folder, forgotten after 30 days. While a run holds the memory lock only new decisions push (`pushNewDecisionsAtOnce`, line 215). Kind: `notifyKindOf` (line 82): the check's group, or `decision with a draft`; `NotifyKind` also has `calendar` (an appointment Life added).

Levels: `NotifyLevel` = `off | quiet | normal | urgent` (`core/names.ts`), set per kind in settings `notifications` (`core/settings.ts:132`), defaults `NOTIFY_DEFAULTS` (`card-push.ts:42`): decision and decision with a draft urgent; calendar quiet; every other kind normal. `levelOf` (line 86): quiet = passive; urgent = time-sensitive only for a card with no deadline or within `URGENT_WITHIN` (3 h) of it, else active; normal = active; off = no push. No words of his go through Apple; the phone writes the text from the connector. Phone side: `apps/lifelog/apps/ios/NotificationService`, `NotificationContent`, `Lifelog/IncidentNotifier.swift` (answer buttons, Send on a draft with unlocked phone/Watch).

Other things that reach him: the nightly email (`host/nightly.ts`, `host/report.ts`: every row, no caps; no ending checks) and Ask Life answers.

## 5. Health and status

Pendant/phone silence: the phone reports at least every 6 h; silent twice as long means the app or its way to lifelog is down (`snapshot.ts:351`).

`read_life_status` (`host/http/mcp.ts:479`, `lifeStatus`, `host/status/life.ts:45`): four clocks, capture (watcher health `derived/watch/health.json`, `polling` within 15 min -> `ok` / `not-polling` / `off` / `unknown`; archive copies per store), extraction, consolidation, index; plus nightly state, `running` while the lock is held. Reads the last 7 days of logs; missing records read `unknown`. Related reads: `read_processing_runs`, `read_memory_changes`, `read_log_lines`, `read_resource_samples` (`host/resource-sample.ts`). Detail: `docs/status-reads.md`.

Agent health: `agentHealthOf` (`host/agent-health.ts:73`) gives `fine | attention | broken` with plain-words `why`, per agent, from `HealthFacts` (amber flags from `amberOf`, `host/agents-now.ts:162`; failed turns in a row, 3 = broken; queued events unheard 30 min = attention, 2 h = broken (`DEAF_AFTER`, `DEAF_BROKEN_AFTER`); session held open elsewhere with wakes waiting 1 h = broken; runtime block and fallback to pi; his Claude app's listing, `host/claude-app-listing.ts`). `deskHealthOf` (line 148, `STANDING_ROLES` main and caretaker) for the desk as a whole. Row builders `host/desk-health.ts` (`healthRowsOf`, `coverageOf`, `heldByTwoOf`, `stoppedOnRunningOf`, `decisionOnIdleMatterOf`, `lastNeedsYouOf`). Exposed on `read_registered_agents` (`host/http/mcp.ts:548`). Agent health lives in Settings diagnostics only; the app shows no agent list or role names.

What notifies the owner when something breaks:
- Mac side, email: the nightly tick (every 30 min) mails every status card in red once per day per problem (`redCards`, `alertKeyOf`, `redReport`, `host/report.ts:365-400`; sent from `host/nightly.ts:~297`). Cards in groups `mac` and `pipeline` are excluded from the alert (`QUIET_GROUPS`), a pipeline run mails itself after 3 tries. A crash loop (5 crashes in 30 min, `host/status/crash-card.ts`) is mailed even after it ends. A failed night: non-zero exit, nothing committed, failure email after the 3rd try. A caretaker feedback mail goes only on his Send of a card (`DEVELOPER_CONTACT`).
- Mac side, banners: `notifyArgs` (`core/platform.ts:127`) shows a macOS notification through osascript (none on Windows); callers are in the app/helper paths (not traced here).
- Phone side: capture incidents posted as local notifications by `IncidentNotifier` (`apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Lifelog/IncidentNotifier.swift`, conditions in `apps/lifelog/apps/ios/Kit/Sources/Incidents/CaptureCondition.swift` and `IncidentReducer.swift`: recording stopped, storage write failed, storage low, no contact, battery low, pendant disconnected, bluetooth off or denied, transfer incomplete, no reply, and so on). These are about the pendant and its capture, as the phone sees them.
- Not notifying today (found by reading the code, no mechanism found): Life or the Mac being unreachable from the phone has no capture condition in `CaptureCondition.swift` and no push (a push needs the Mac to send it); the Voice host (lifelog) being unreachable appears only as the lifelog status card and, if red, the next email, which also needs the Mac; offline (his model provider unreachable, `isOnline`, `models/llm.ts:538`) makes the nightly tick run nothing and send no mail (`host/nightly.ts:249`); `docs/operations.md` says "nothing alerts on an outage" for voice delivery. A desk agent going broken has no push; it shows only in Settings diagnostics and `read_registered_agents`. An amber or idle card does not email.

## 6. Spending

Recorded: each record that used a paid or metered resource is read as one `Spend` where it is logged (`memory/spends.ts`, decision 83): `spendsOfLine` (`model.run` and `model.local` log lines), `spendsOfTurn` / `spendOfTurn` (desk turns: models, and web searches apart), `spendOfApifyRun` (a card's `ran` line, Apify's bill), `spendsOfVoice` (Voice's `GET /spending`, `sources/lifelog-spending.ts`). `SpendKind`: night, daytime, agents, web_search, apify, voice. `SpendProvider`: openrouter, allmodels, elevenlabs, claude, codex, describer, apple, speech, apify, web_search. `providerOfRuntime` (line 31): codex -> codex, pi -> openrouter, else claude. Prices: `core/prices.ts` (`priceOn`, dated rows, `HOUR_PRICES`, `listUsdOf`, `requestUsdNow`). A turn's model name is `turnModelOf` = `<runtime>:<model|default>` (`host/desk-read.ts:564`).

## 7. Reminders, to-dos, recurring behaviour

To-do list: one backend per install, `todoList` setting (`core/settings.ts:183`): Apple Reminders (`todo-list/remctl.ts`) or Google Tasks (`todo-list/google.ts`), picked by `backend()` (`todo-list/backend.ts`); contract and `Can` capabilities (`alarms`, `recurrence`, `sections`, `urgent`, subtasks...) in `todo-list/contract.ts`. All writes go through `changeTodoItem` (`host/change-todo-item.ts`) on his request (connector `change_todo_item`, `host/http/mcp.ts`, or the main session's door) or the auto-add permission for a to-do he said aloud (`obs`); the ledger is the truth (`todo-list/todo-items-from-ledger.ts`). Reads: `read_open_todo_items`. Items have title, notes, due day, list, section, `urgent`, `recurrence` where the backend can. Google Tasks has no time and no alarms (`docs/tasks.md` ~72). The word "reminder" in the code refers to this backend and to a card's calendar popup reminders (event need: 1 to 3, `memory/needs.ts`).

What the owner can set today for recurring or scheduled behaviour: an agent's wait `until` (up to 7 days ahead, then the default wait), a card's deadline (1 h to 14 days) and `onSilence` option, the owner-chosen settings `desk.handOut` pace and `notifications` levels. No owner-facing mechanism to create a recurring or scheduled task or a timed reminder of his own was found in the server code: the schedules that exist are Life's own (`memory/schedule.ts` nightly and its tick; watcher timers: 5 min units, 1 min cards, hourly pictures, 30 s withdrawals) and calendar writes (appointments Life reads from messages, an agent's event on his `adds` yes, the Past calendar: `host/google-calendar.ts`, `host/appointments-calendar.ts`, `memory/arrival-appointments.ts`). `desk report-line` posts a line for the nightly's Agents section from a scheduled task or session (`desk/desk-helper.ts`, `ReportLinePosted`). Scheduled tasks outside Life (the Claude Code environment's) are not part of Life's code.
