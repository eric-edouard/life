waves. Written 26 Nov 2026 on the owner's ask, in ultrafast mode: bundles up to about 3,000 lines, tests only for what is
critical, the rest checked by running it and owed in [`tests-owed.md`](tests-owed.md). Bundles on strict paths
(`AGENTS.md`: the desk, the connector, send paths, memory writers, prompts) keep fast mode's verdict rank, per the
owner's rule of 25 Nov: core work that will not change goes through fast. v4's plan and its reasons stay in
[`2026-11-25-overhaul/`](2026-11-25-overhaul/README.md).

Not here: what waits on the owner (the prototype's 11 design decisions, one real Undo per network, the aura's
settings) and the work after v4 (#1379 `truth/` to `stated/`, a project's "about" line, Codex on demand, #727 duplicate
people, the Nod items #1380–#1383 and #981). Each gets its own plan.

## What each builds

**G, agents and the main session**
- An `intention` instruction opens its assignment: the hand-out tick reads intentions in force and opens one per
  intention with no open assignment, on the agent `agentForOf` names (A recorded them, nothing opened them).
- An agent's packet holds the records of the other open assignments under the same project (their subject, plan steps,
  last note line), read-only. Never their sessions.
- A question's reply after 60 s lands as a thread entry that follows the ask (`follows`), instead of being dropped: the
  question stays answerable until its assignment's next turn ends.
- The main session's turn has a limit (5 minutes); past it the run is cancelled with C's `cancel`, the entry ends with
  "I couldn't finish that", and the queue moves on.
- The spending stop is checked inside a turn too: pi's extension reads the work's spend after each tool call and ends
  the turn at the stop, its why `budget`.
- Life offers a setting at the moment it helps: the main brief gains one line, and the `settings` block may carry one
  `offer` (a single switch), shown once per setting until he answers or dismisses it.
- The wire says whether a redraft came from a tap or from words (`changed: ["edit:tap" | "edit:words"]`).

**H, delivery**
- Hold a reply until his usual reply time: from the exchange counts (`memory/exchange-counts.ts`), his median reply
  hour per chat; a draft card into a chat he answers in batches is held to that hour, never past a deadline, never more
  than 12 h. Code only; the gate's readings are unchanged.
- "How am I doing?": at most once a day, at a moment the owner state reads free, one thread item with three marks and
  dismiss (`rated`, D's line). Dismiss and silence count for nothing.
- The app is open counts as "free" in the owner state (the desk's last ask from the phone within 2 minutes).
- Later "when I'm home": a Later held until the next arrival at his home place (the arrival events E reads), else 24 h.

**J, deletion reaches v4's copies** (a step of #1384, not all of it)
- `deleteFromMemory`'s preview and write include thread entries whose words or answer hold the deleted words or cite a
  deleted ref, blanked in place (`deleted_at` set, words and answer emptied, the row kept), and instruction lines
  quoting them (a `deleted` correction applied at read by `instructionsFor`, the file never rewritten).
- `SECURITY.md` says what deletion now reaches and what still holds the words (lifelog, the archive, backups, history).
- Critical tests: the preview lists the entries and instructions; the write blanks them; a blanked entry reads empty.

**P4, the app's gaps**
- A person picker for `takes: person` options (his directory's people, searched by name).
- Notification answers go through the thread's read, and Kit's `Check`, `Matter`, `RegisteredAgent` and `MatterRow`
  models and the `read_inbox` path go.
- Draws G's settings offer and late reply, and H's "How am I doing?" item, simple and easy to restyle until his design
  decisions.
